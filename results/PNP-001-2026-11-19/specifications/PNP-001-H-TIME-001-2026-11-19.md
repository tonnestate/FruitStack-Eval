# PNP-001 Research Assignment H-TIME-001 — Time models, Round-Robin scheduling and information reduction in graph-based computation

Owner assignment 2026-11-19 (binding specification; research and verification only — NOT authorisation to redesign FruitStack architecture,
mission control, routing or proof validation).

## Headline question
Which information must be preserved when a dependency graph is built and filtered step by step, and when can different search branches be
merged without loss?

## Objective
Investigate whether the temporal organisation of computation (processor time slicing, Round-Robin, concurrency, order of information
propagation) changes the amount of computation necessary for combinatorial decision problems — without assuming conventional complexity is
wrongly defined. Determine which aspects of time are model-dependent, which invariant, and whether scheduling changes work or only redistributes it.

## Primary research question
Can temporal scheduling of branching, joining, filtering and shared information propagation in a computational dependency graph reduce the
TOTAL amount of necessary computation, beyond processor-level parallel speedup?

## 1. Time models (exact definitions; where equivalent / comparable / different)
physical time; processor service time; Round-Robin time (quanta, preemption, context switches, waiting, resumption, overhead); parallel time
(bounded processors; total work vs critical path); logical time (causal order); statistical time (distributions, expectation, tail, variance,
WCET); complexity-theoretic time (elementary steps vs encoded input size). Physical relativity / observation effects only as conceptual
comparison, never as a speedup claim without a valid mathematical mapping.

## 2. Round Robin as research variable
Separate (a) OS Round Robin (preemptive time sharing, quantum) and (b) graph-search Round Robin (cyclic allocation among active branches /
subproblems / propagation tasks) — not automatically equivalent. Measure the influence of quantum, interruption order, processor count, shared
memory, synchronisation, information exchange on: useful operations; context-switch and scheduling overhead; time to contradiction/solution;
branches explored; branches eliminated by propagated information; reuse of intermediate results; peak memory; total completion time.
A single time-sliced processor is never counted as several simultaneous processors.

## 3. Graph-tree computation (exact semantics)
Dependency graph with branching, join, filter, projection/compression (preserving all decision-relevant information), information sharing.
Principal problem: positive 1-in-3-SAT. No satisfying assignment may be lost; UNSAT must have an independently checkable justification.
For formula F, algorithm A, policy pi, processors p, quantum q measure W (work), D (critical depth), T (completion time), M (peak memory),
H (scheduling/synchronisation overhead). Separate fixed execution graphs from policies that change the graph (early stopping, propagation,
learning, pruning).

## 4. Reproducible comparison
Same instances and correctness requirements for: sequential DFS; sequential BFS; Round-Robin over active branches; bounded processor pool with
work stealing. Deterministic simulator with counted logical operations (isolating scheduling from hardware) AND real measurements (CPU
utilisation, preemption overhead, memory). Several quanta and processor counts; resources and budgets recorded. Instances: SAT and UNSAT
positive 1-in-3; GF(3)-blind cores; certified n=8 cores (dataset agent_director_cg_certificates_n8_v1); whole formulas with extra variables
and constraints; controls 2-SAT and XOR-SAT; n=9 results only after validation. Identical instances, controlled seeds, repeated trials;
algorithm changes kept separate from scheduling-only changes.

## 5. Hypothesis H-TIME-001 (scheduling-sensitive information reduction)
Temporal interleaving may let information found in one branch eliminate work in others, reducing executed computation, not only wall-clock
time. Test against a fixed-work control. Attribute any reduction to: utilisation; reduced waiting/synchronisation; earlier contradiction;
better constraint sharing; smaller intermediate representations; avoided duplicates; genuine search-tree reduction. Do not credit a new time
model where branching order, memoisation or constraint learning already explains it.

## 6. Mathematical limits
Compare with Brent's work-depth principle, Round-Robin scheduling theory, work-stealing bounds, SAT branching-order results. Distinguish
scheduling a FIXED computation (order cannot reduce fixed useful work) from scheduling an ADAPTIVE computation (order can change which
operations are ever needed). Prove or refute a precisely scoped statement about the adaptive case; if an advantage exists, give conditions,
resource accounting, restriction to graph classes/algorithms. No general lower bound on arbitrary polynomial-time algorithms from bounds on a
scheduling or search mechanism.

## 7. Required results (one consolidated, auditable report)
exact definitions of all time models; formal Round-Robin model; reproducible traces (branch creation, interruption, resumption, filtering,
elimination); controlled measurements of W, T, D, H, M; at least one precise claim with proof, counterexample or documented OPEN_UNPROVEN;
comparison with work-depth, scheduling and SAT-search theory; assessment of what goes beyond established algorithms; the exact remaining
logical gap to any P-vs-NP result.

## Acceptance
Success = a new verified mathematical relationship, a rigorously characterised algorithmic effect, or a decisive counterexample. Faster
wall-clock time alone is insufficient; fewer explored branches count only after all scheduling, sharing and preprocessing costs. No
assumption of P != NP or P = NP replaces a proof.

## Director's starting points (2026-11-19, to be checked, not assumed)
- Lossless merge criterion: branches alpha, beta (partial assignments on processed variables) are mergeable iff F|alpha == F|beta as Boolean
  functions of the unprocessed variables (Myhill-Nerode type); equal satisfiability is NOT sufficient. Minimal state = residual-function
  class; per-step count = OBDD/DP width. For positive 1-in-3 under a variable order: residual determined by the frontier clauses (partly
  assigned) and whether each already has a true variable -> width <= 2^(#frontier clauses) (cut-width bound). Classical, not new.
- A GF(3) syndrome summary of a branch is a LOSSY merge; the GF(3)-blind formulas are exactly where it merges branches that must stay
  apart; D-018 names the missing information (two-valuedness).
- Simulation: any deterministic interleaving on p processors is simulated sequentially with work <= W(pi) + H(pi); a scheduling gain is the
  gain of a particular sequential search strategy. Brent: T_p <= W/p + D for fixed computations.
- Limits: interleaving with shared learned clauses = portfolio CDCL ~ resolution; exponential resolution lower bounds (pigeonhole, Tseitin on
  expanders) bound every schedule of such methods; tree-like DPLL bounds hold for every branching order.

## Owner corrections 2026-11-19 (binding; supersede the director's starting points above where they conflict)
1. For UNSAT F, F|alpha == 0 for every alpha: the semantic (Myhill-Nerode) residual count is 1 for every cut and order; the reduced OBDD
   is the 0-terminal. Semantic width can NOT grow on UNSAT GAP cores. Hardness = cost of CONSTRUCTING that representation (OBDD-based
   refutation lower bounds exist although the final diagram has one node). Ledger: H-TIME-001a and H-INFO-001 retracted as SUPERSEDED,
   continued as H-CONSTRUCT-001 (status OPEN_UNPROVEN).
2. A GF(3) summary is not lossy per se: per positive 1-in-3 clause, residual sum mod 3 together with the number of still unassigned
   variables reconstructs the exact Boolean residual condition. Loss appears only under a further abstraction (dropping the Boolean value
   restriction and keeping only solvability of the linear GF(3) system). Any loss claim must name the exact summary and the processing step.
   D-018 is global; it does not yet say at which step a given summary loses information.
3. Large OBDD width does not imply a hard decision: monotone 2-CNF families exist whose solution function needs exponential OBDDs under
   every order although 2-SAT is polynomial. 2-SAT/XOR are therefore no "small-width" controls for semantic width.
Central measure: W_construct(F, A, pi) = number of distinct intermediate representations, equivalence checks and elementary operations
needed by procedure A under order/schedule pi to reach a correct decision or a checkable UNSAT certificate.
Central question: why can the finally needed information be tiny while computing it may require exponential work?

## Owner refinement 2026-11-19 (second round, binding)
- Order dependence of intermediate OBDD sizes is ELEMENTARY and known, not a result: F0 = {abc, abd, acd, bcd} (each variable in 3 clauses,
  3(a+b+c+d) = 4 impossible) plus an independent satisfiable structure G on disjoint variables: F0 first -> 0 immediately; G first -> large
  intermediate diagrams, same final result. (Director note: F0 is also GF(3)-inconsistent, 3*sum = 0 != 1 mod 3; for blind instances take
  a certified blind core instead of F0 - same argument.)
- Measure separately: M_max = max_t |OBDD_t| (memory), W = sum_t cost(operation_t) (work), T_wall (elapsed time). Equal M_max does not
  imply equal W or T_wall.
- Existential elimination of a variable is admissible only when no unprocessed constraint still depends on it (all clauses containing it
  already conjoined).
- The scientific part starts with the OPTIMAL construction cost over all admissible join / projection / merge orders.

## Owner addition 2026-11-19 (final; no further reformulation)
- W must include the real cost of every equivalence decision used for merging. Equivalence of arbitrary formulas is coNP-complete; treating
  it as free moves the difficulty into the merge step. In the OBDD model, equivalence of reduced OBDDs under one order is a pointer/hash
  comparison only because canonicity is paid for in apply/reduce - those operations count in W. Any other merge test is charged at its
  actual cost.
- Distinguish: compact result (constant 0) / compact representation (one terminal) / efficient construction (separate question).
- Goal: for an exactly defined computation model, which intermediate steps are unavoidable even with optimised branching order, Round Robin,
  projection and merging. An exponential bound for OBDD-based procedures is a result about that class only, not a proof of P != NP.
