## title

Corrected exhaustive one-pass EXT decision bound

## mathematical_statement

Fix n>=1 and s>=0. Consider Boolean circuits on n inputs with at most s fan-in-two AND/OR/NOT gates, constants 0 and 1, and one designated output. There is a deterministic one-pass algorithm deciding whether each streamed truth-table prefix extends to the truth table of a circuit in this class. It stores at most D=(s+1)(n+s+2)(3(n+s+2)^2)^s candidate bits, uses O(Ds+n+s) elementary operations per streamed bit and O(D+n+s) working bits. For circuits with exactly j gates the number of descriptions is at most (n+j+2)(3(n+j+2)^2)^j, and summing over 0<=j<=s gives D. A live bit remains iff the prefix has a completion in the class. The bound is exponential, gives no polynomial update bound, and gives no lower bound for arbitrary representations.

It stores at most D=(s+1)(n+s+2)(3(n+s+2)^2)^s candidate bits and uses O(Ds+n+s) elementary operations per streamed bit and O(D+n+s) working bits. Proof: For circuits with exactly j gates, enumerate topological descriptions. At gate i there are at most three gate types and at most (n+i+2)^2 ordered predecessor choices, counting the n inputs, two constants, and earlier gates. There are at most n+j+2 choices for the output wire. Thus the number of descriptions with j gates is at most (n+j+2)(3(n+j+2)^2)^j. Summing over 0≤j≤s gives at most (s+1)(n+s+2)(3(n+s+2)^2)^s descriptions. Store one live bit per description. Initially all are live. At position i, evaluate each live circuit on the i-th input, clearing its bit when its output differs from the streamed bit. Inductively, after each prefix, exactly the descriptions whose truth tables begin with that prefix remain live; hence a live bit remains iff the prefix has a completion in the circuit class. Evaluating each circuit costs O(s+n) operations including indexing the input, yielding the stated per-bit bound.
