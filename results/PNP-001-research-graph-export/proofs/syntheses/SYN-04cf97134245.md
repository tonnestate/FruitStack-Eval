## title

Counting bound on descriptions of size-at-most-s circuits

## mathematical_statement

Fix n>=1 and s>=0. In the model of topologically ordered circuits with n designated input wires, at most s gates each chosen from AND, OR, NOT with fan-in at most two, and one designated output wire, the number M of circuit descriptions is at most (s+1)(n+s)(3(n+s)^2)^s. The explicit incidence-vector candidate representation therefore has an update circuit of size at most M+1, hence at most (s+1)(n+s)(3(n+s)^2)^s+1. This does not establish a polynomial update bound or a lower bound for other representations.

## original_proof

proved_step: statement: LEMMA (explicit circuit-list size bound). Fix n>=1 and s>=0. In the model of topologically ordered circuits with n designated input wires, at most s gates each chosen from AND, OR, NOT (with fan-in at most two), and one designated output wire, the number M of circuit descriptions is at most (s+1)(n+s)(3(n+s)^2)^s. Proof: For a circuit with exactly j gates, each gate has at most 3(n+s)^2 choices: one of three gate types and an ordered pair of sources among at most n+s available wires (this overcounts unary NOT gates and allows unused later wires, so is an upper bound). The output has at most n+s choices. Thus there are at most (n+s)(3(n+s)^2)^j descriptions with j gates. Summing j=0,...,s and using (3(n+s)^2)^j <= (3(n+s)^2)^s gives the stated bound. Consequently the explicit incidence-vector candidate representation has an update circuit of size at most M+1, hence at most (s+1)(n+s)(3(n+s)^2)^s+1. This quantifies the representation-specific cost and does not establish a polynomial update bound or a lower bound for other representations.
