## mathematical_statement

Fix n,s and an explicitly listed set C of D Boolean circuits on n inputs, each with at most s fan-in-two gates. Maintain a D-bit vector a with a_C=1 exactly when candidate C agrees with all truth-table prefix bits seen so far, and let i be the address of the next row. On receiving bit b, update a'_C = a_C AND (b iff C(i)). This update is implementable by a fan-in-two AND/OR/NOT circuit of O(D(s+1)) gates; if the n-bit address is maintained internally, add O(n) gates for its increment.
