## title

Residual-width bound for a finite candidate family

## mathematical_statement

Let F be a nonempty family of K distinct binary strings of length N. For 0<=t<=N and each length-t prefix u let R_u={v in {0,1}^(N-t): uv in F}, and let q_t be the number of distinct nonempty sets R_u. Then q_t <= min(K, 2^t, 2^(2^(N-t))). Consequence: the exact level-t residual automaton can store its active state in one-hot form with q_t bits, with O(q_t) fan-in-two gates per binary transition. The record states the G0004 gap remains open and that the bound does not give a polynomial-in-(s,n) update for EXT_{n,s} or arbitrary H_C.

## original_proof

proved_step: LEMMA (residual-width bound for a finite candidate family). Let F be a nonempty family of K distinct binary strings of length N. For 0<=t<=N, for each length-t prefix u define R_u={v in {0,1}^{N-t}: uv in F}; let q_t be the number of distinct nonempty sets R_u. Then q_t <= min(K, 2^t, 2^(2^(N-t))). Proof: There are at most 2^t prefixes u, hence at most 2^t residuals. Every nonempty residual contains at least one suffix v from a member uv of F; choosing one such member for each distinct residual gives distinct members of F, since distinct residual sets cannot be witnessed by the same member's prefix group. Thus q_t<=K. Finally each residual is a subset of the 2^(N-t) possible suffixes, so there are at most 2^(2^(N-t)) possible residual sets. Taking all three bounds proves the claim. Consequence: the exact level-t residual automaton can store its active state in one-hot form using q_t bits (plus a rejecting sink if desired); its binary transition at that level can be implemented by routing each source state/input pair to the unique next residual or sink, with O(q_t) fan-in-two gates. This sharpens the candidate-list representation to a bound controlled by distinct residuals, but q_t can still be as large as K and the result does not give a polynomial-in-(s,n) update for EXT_{n,s} or arbitrary MCSP circuits.
gap: The assigned G0004 / EXT-to-H_C transition-cost gap remains open. The residual-width inequality only bounds update cost by q_t<=K; it does not show q_t is polynomial in s,n, nor express the transition through a bounded number of real gates on arbitrary MCSP decision circuits H_C. The exhaustive N=4 discovery check is pending and is not a proof.
