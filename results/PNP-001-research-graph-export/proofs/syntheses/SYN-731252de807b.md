## title

Explicit-list EXT update through residual OR gates

## mathematical_statement

Let F={C_1,...,C_M} be an explicitly listed family of Boolean circuits on n inputs with truth tables E_j in a fixed order. For a prefix p of length t<2^n define A={j: E_j[0..t-1]=p}. Then EXT_F(p0)=OR_{j in A}[E_j[t]=0] and EXT_F(p1)=OR_{j in A}[E_j[t]=1]. Both bits have fan-in-two circuits of size O(M(t+1)(s+n)). This is an enumeration upper bound; it does not bound M polynomially or implement the update from an arbitrary fixed H_C.
