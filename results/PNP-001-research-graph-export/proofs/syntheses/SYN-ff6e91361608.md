## title

One-hot transition gate bound for explicit prefix automata

## mathematical_statement

For A subset {0,1}^N with |A|=M>=1, the prefix automaton of Pref(A), with a rejecting sink, has q <= 1+sum_{k=0}^N min(2^k,M) states and admits a one-hot binary-input transition circuit with at most 4q+1 fan-in-two AND/OR/NOT gates. The construction computes NOT b once, forms x_i AND NOT b and x_i AND b for each state (2q gates), routes each signal to its unique successor, and ORs signals per destination (at most 2q gates).

proved_step: statement: LEMMA (explicit-prefix transition gate bound). For A⊆{0,1}^N with |A|=M≥1, the prefix automaton of Pref(A), with a rejecting sink, has q≤1+Σ_{k=0}^N min(2^k,M) states and admits a one-hot binary-input transition circuit with at most 4q+1 fan-in-two AND/OR/NOT gates.
derivation: At depth k there are at most 2^k binary prefixes and at most M distinct prefixes occurring among the M words, so there are at most min(2^k,M) nonempty-prefix states arising at that depth. Summing over depths is an upper bound even if states coincide across depths; add at most one empty residual sink. For the transition, let x_i indicate one-hot state i. Compute not b once. For every i form x_i AND not b and x_i AND b, costing 2q gates. Each of the 2q signals is routed to the one-hot bit of its unique successor. OR all routed signals at each destination; the total number of signals across destination lists is 2q, so binary OR trees use at most 2q gates. The resulting valid one-hot vector is the unique successor. Total ≤4q+1.
