Then the exact next state is z'_i=z_i AND c_i for every i, so each next-state coordinate is either the input wire z_i or the constant 0 and the transition needs zero fan-in-two gates when wires and constants are free. The acceptance readout OR_i z_i takes K-1 fan-in-two OR gates for K>=1 (zero gates for K=1).
