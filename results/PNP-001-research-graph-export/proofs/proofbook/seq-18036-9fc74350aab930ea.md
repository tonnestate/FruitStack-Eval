T50 (theory/T50-external-vpvnp-mechanism-and-boolean-transfer-2026-11-27.md): mechanism analysis of UNVERIFIED external VP!=VNP proofs (OpenAI Nov 23, Anthropic). Kept separate from established results; only the overviews have been read; Lean verification is pending. Owner decision recorded: T48 is consolidated, Q-BD is closed as a route, and the global contradiction mechanism is the focus.
Mechanism (both papers): a win-win dichotomy on a geometric hypothesis about tensor prices (spectral points/wire lengths). Flat case: rigidity, i.e. small rank across balanced cuts, gives a direct bound. Contracting case: wire saving lets a hypothetical small circuit be replayed faster, giving an accelerator (v^H steps in v^{H-16}) that iterates; a diagonal argument shows there are none (thm_noacc). The circuit enters only in two steps (arrows C: lem_balanced, lem_lowdeg), which use the block-multilinear and low-degree parts of the gate polynomials.
Boolean break point (B1): Boolean correctness lives in F[x]/I, which is only filtered, not graded (x^2 -> x). A Boolean circuit computes g = f + sum (x_i^2-x_i)h_i (Anthropic sec.2.5; = T49 Lemma3, T48 sec.3). The graded parts of g are not those of f; the explicit h_i (T48 8.1/9.1) do not help.
Required bridge MT (multilinearization transfer): a Boolean circuit of size s for multilinear f over F2 gives an arithmetic circuit of size poly(s) for f. NF(g) is always in VNP_2. (B2) MT plus the external theorem would only give parity-P not in P/poly (HC over F2 = parity of #HC; Valiant criterion, from memory), and P=NP is not known to imply parity-P in P/poly. Counting vs existence: no tool is known. (B3) The Boolean analogue of the accelerator diagonal needs a fixed exponent saving; P=NP gives only NTIME(t) in DTIME(t^c) with c>=1. The known Boolean speedup/slowdown contradictions are alternation trading (T27, closed).
First unproved implication on this route: MT. Together with the external theorem it is itself a Boolean lower bound, so it is not a small step, and it would still be parity-P, not NP. Next steps: Lean builds plus #print axioms, then the appendices (lem_balanced, lem_lowdeg, thm_noacc).
