## Statement / proof text (verbatim)

For positive 1-in-3 CNF in which every variable lies in at most 2 clauses, the formula is satisfiable iff there is a matching in the clause multigraph (nodes = clauses, edges = variables lying in exactly 2 clauses) that covers every clause containing no variable of occurrence 1. Consequence: the max-occ<=2 class is decided in polynomial time by matching, and every minimal unsatisfiable core in this class is a matching obstruction. Counting 3m = sum of occurrences <= 2n gives m <= 2n/3.
