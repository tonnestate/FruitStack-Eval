## Statement / proof text (verbatim)

For an integer m x n matrix A of full row rank m (here m=6 with n in {6,7}), Ax=1 has an integer solution iff gcd of the m x m minors of A equals gcd of the m x m minors of [A|1]. For the 30 infeasible n=6 sets, gcd of minors of A should be 3 and that of [A|1] should be 1.
