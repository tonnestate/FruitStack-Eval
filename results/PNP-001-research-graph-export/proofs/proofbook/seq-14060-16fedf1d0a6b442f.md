## Statement / proof text (verbatim)

The CYCLE-DECOMPOSITION ENCODING BOUND (S0534) shows that any single permutation can be updated in O(SN / L_min) gates where L_min is the shortest cycle length. For the lower bound Omega(sqrt(m)/log m) to hold generally, hard permutations must have L_min = O(sqrt(m)) or the bound must come from pairs of permutations (Claim B) rather than single permutations. This suggests: (1) the single-permutation lower bound Omega(sqrt(m)/log m) may only apply to permutations with all cycles of length at least Omega(sqrt(m)), or (2) the hard instance for the single-permutation bound uses a specific cycle structure that forces L_min small but the encoding cannot exploit the cycle decomposition due to the stationary encoding constraint. Need to test whether there exist permutations with L_min = 1 that still require Omega(sqrt(m)/log m) gates under EVERY encoding.
