## Statement / proof text (verbatim)

T13-1/2 (asymptotic, primary-source verified): define uniform HWV covariant families P_{T_n}(coeff(f))=<HWV(T_n), f^{tensor d(n)}> and separate three complexities (BDI21): construction, REPRESENTATION (circuit size of P_{T_n}), EVALUATION at a specific f. BDI21 VERBATIM: evaluating HWVs is NP-hard in general, but efficient when the TREEWIDTH of the Young-tableau is small (point encoded as noncommutative ABP), and efficiently evaluable at points of low (border) Waring rank. KEY STRUCTURAL CONCLUSION: my T11 results (d<=4) live in the BOUNDED-TREEWIDTH / VP-regime that the GKSS/FSV natural-proofs barrier COVERS (conditional on succinct hitting sets for VP), which partially explains why no obstruction was found there. A barrier-breaking GCT obstruction MUST use GROWING-TREEWIDTH families where P_{T_n} leaves VP (NP-hard general evaluation), while perm/det's low Waring rank still allows efficient evaluation at the target (BDI21). Barrier is CONDITIONAL (succinct hitting sets open; CKRST show VP-natural proofs exist in some settings) - 'P_{T_n} in VP' does not by itself kill the approach. T13-3/4 (open frontier, not computational): the exact treewidth threshold for P_{T_n} not in VP, and whether growing-treewidth lambda(n) keeps det-vanishing + low-Waring-rank efficient perm evaluation.
