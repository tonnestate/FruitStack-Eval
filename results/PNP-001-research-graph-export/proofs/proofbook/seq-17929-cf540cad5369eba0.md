This is the owner's 'algorithm graph H_n'. T43 sec. 4: the uniform bridge P != NP would need is a P-uniform, scaled-down algorithmic method (nontrivial SAT algorithm against P-uniform circuits plus diagonalization inside NP); not known. 17143: exclusion criterion 'black-box bound with no white-box transfer'. 17359: P != NP is uniform, natural proofs concern circuit lower bounds, relativization and algebrization concern every uniform route. (2) What the construction forces (PI). Under P = NP, a P-uniform circuit family C_n computes the selection S (Fable Prop E). H_n = phi_{C_n} gives U_{H_n}(X,Y) = sum over inputs x of X^x Y^{comp(x)}: one monomial per input. The only relation forced between U_G and U_{H_n} is correctness: the output part of the monomial for input G is a monomial of U_G, or the UNSAT symbol iff U_G = 0. The internal gates are unconstrained beyond correctness and uniformity. Any further forced property of the gates is a P-uniform circuit lower bound for S, which is equivalent to P != NP (T45 Lemma T5). (3) Barrier placement. White-box use of the gates avoids relativization only if a step uses the gate structure. An algebraic analysis of U_{H_n} (arithmetization of the computation) is the setting of Aaronson-Wigderson algebrization (cited from memory, verify at source): such steps algebrize unless they use more than low-degree extensions. A property derived from truth tables of the circuits must avoid the natural-proofs conditions (largeness, constructivity). (4) Unused joint consequence (candidate, owner to decide): T43 Q-BD asks whether satisfiability of Lemma-B images phi_C with functional backdoor X (|X| = n) is decidable in 2^n / n^{omega(1)} poly(|phi|). The hybrid elimination of 17919/17921 is exactly a tool for that structure: clauses are linear equations mod 3, the rest is forced by the backdoor. A saving gives NEXP not in P/poly via Williams (not P != NP; T43). This is a concrete point where the new mathematics meets an existing bridge without a lower-bound premise. No obligation closed.
