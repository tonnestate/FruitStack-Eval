## Statement / proof text (verbatim)

ADDRESSED TABLEAU W(t,i) + ALGEBRAIC EVALUATION UNDER P=NP (owner 2026-11-27; PI analysis; CLAIMED, Claude family; literature from memory). (1) Small circuits for W(t,i) of an EXP machine is the premise EXP subset P/poly (Meyer/Kannan route, PI reply 2026-11-27). Its algebraic version, the low-degree extension of the tableau, is Babai-Fortnow-Lund: EXP subset P/poly => EXP = MA. Under P=NP, MA subset Sigma2 = P, so P=NP => EXP not in P/poly. That is known, and it is no contradiction because the premise is not established (E subset SIZE(2^{o(n)}) contradicts the Impagliazzo-Wigderson hypothesis). (2) Which evaluations ev_S(U_G) does P=NP make polynomial? Boolean (SAT): yes. Tropical with poly-bit weights (min energy, an optimization in P^NP): yes. Approximate counting/marginals (Stockmeyer, in PH): yes. Exact N, Z (#P), F_2 (parity-P), F_p (Mod_pP), C: not known; Toda gives PH subset P^{#P}, the opposite direction. Hence the assumption P=NP does not unlock the exact algebraic evaluations of 17561/17584, so they cannot enter an 'assume P=NP, build a fast algorithm' contradiction as efficient subroutines. No obligation closed.
