## Statement / proof text (verbatim)

Transfer limit of the OGP -> circuit route (Q-5c20e15028). The Linial-Mansour-Nisan step needs the target Boolean function f to have Fourier weight concentrated on low degree. For AC0 circuits of size s and depth d, the weight above degree (log s)^{O(d)} is at most 1/poly, so the route can reach constant-depth classes. For general poly-size circuits there is no such concentration (parity has all its weight at degree n), so the route cannot reach P/poly through Fourier concentration alone. The limit is therefore the Fourier-concentration property of the circuit class, not the OGP property of the instance.
