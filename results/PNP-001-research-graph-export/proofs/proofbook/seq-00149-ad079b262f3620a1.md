## Statement / proof text (verbatim)

For char != 2, dim K[X]/I_h(a,b) = max(a,b)(h-1) for 1<=h<=min(a,b), h<max(a,b). Consequently codim Crit(perm_n) = 2n, i.e. dim Sigma(perm_n) = n(n-2); for n=5, dim Sigma(perm_5)=15. The dimension is realized by the family supported on h-1=n-2 rows (the '2-zero-rows' family for n=5, which has Hessian rank 2n=10). Xie explicitly states the dimension calc does NOT imply reducedness; the complete-intersection/reduced result (Thm 1.3) is ONLY for maximal permanents I_k(k,k+1), NOT for the square critical locus I_{n-1}(n,n). No equidimensionality or minimal-component-dimension statement.
