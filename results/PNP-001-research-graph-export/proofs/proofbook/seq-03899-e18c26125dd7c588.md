## Statement / proof text (verbatim)

BEYOND PC (owner deep research 2026-11-19, director checks): (1) IPS TRANSFER SCHEMA: in GF(2)[x,y] (no multilinearisation) the substitution y -> 1+a+b maps L_edge to 0, q_edge to ab + (a^2+a) + (b^2+b), y^2+y to (a^2+a)+(b^2+b) (sympy-checked); vertex axioms map to colouring axioms. Substituting a 3-term affine form into an ARITHMETIC CIRCUIT costs only additive size, so a size-s circuit-coefficient IPS refutation of F(G) gives a size-O(s+|G|) IPS refutation of the 3-colouring axioms of G (formal size statement still to be fixed). Unlike roABP-IPS (order-sensitive), general IPS has no width obstacle here. (2) Grochow-Pitassi 2014: any superpolynomial IPS lower bound implies VP != VNP - so an IPS lower bound for F(G) is at least as hard as VP != VNP; the transfer shows F(G) is no easier than 3-colouring, it does not provide the bound. (3) Hardness magnification (Oliveira-Pich-Santhanam): slightly superlinear bounds for Gap-MCSP would give NP not in P/poly; director note: magnification is limited by the LOCALITY BARRIER (Chen-Hirahara-Oliveira-Pich-Rajgopal-Santhanam 2020) - known lower-bound techniques extend to oracle-augmented circuits and cannot supply the needed bounds. No direct link from our 1-in-3 results. (4) Quantifier gap stays explicit: hard instances for a proof system say nothing about every circuit computing SAT_N. (5) Mishra-Santhanam 4n - O(log n): still only verified by the director as a STOC 2027 submission claim.
