This closes the open lower bound of 17911 (6c): [L_H : M_H] = 3^r exactly, not only 3^{rank_F3}. Proof outline (Fable): (1) Kempe swaps of H are global transpositions (17911 (6b)). So M_H = {sum delta_sigma n_sigma : delta_sigma in the augmentation ideal of Z[S3]}, and L_H / M_H = W / (W cap M_H), where W is the span of n_sigma - n_sigma0. (2) W is isomorphic to L_G via the injective linear gadget map x_sigma to n_sigma. (3) w in M_H iff there are a_sigma in Z[S3] with augmentation c_sigma and sum a_sigma n_sigma = 0. (4) Per vertex v put B_c = sum over sigma with sigma(v) = c of a_sigma, and M_c = m(B_c). Vanishing on the 7 gadget vertices and on R forces every M_c = (C_{v,c}/3) J, so 3 | C. (5) B_c = (C_c/3) E + t_c sgn. Read off at pi: C/3 + T for even pi and -T for odd pi, so C/3 is in L_G. The converse uses a_sigma = c'_sigma E. Hence W cap M_H corresponds to 3 L_G. PI check: step 4 is a finite local statement, the same for every v, and is decided by an exact rational kernel computation. Gadget table read off build_H, matching Fable's table up to row order: (012), (121), (202), (010), (122), (200), (011). The relation module has dimension 5 (2 from s_c with sum 0, 3 from sgn), and every M_c in it has equal columns (scratchpad/prism/thmH.py). Integrality of entries gives 3 | C_{v,c}. Steps 1, 2, 3 and 5 were checked by hand. Consistent with the exact index computations on 15 graphs (17911). Meaning: the Kempe lattice of Prism(G) carries no information beyond L_G mod 3.
