## Statement / proof text (verbatim)

D6'' (repair attempt for R', conditional): surviving narrow reused gates need not be paid in CORRELATION (AF budget N^{beta-eta}); they can be paid in formula SIZE. After the restriction a reused gate g with k_g live variables computes a function of k_g variables (formula size <= 2^{k_g}); substitute it at each of its f_g occurrences. Expected added size sum_g f_g E[2^{k_g} 1(k_g >= 2)] ~ sum_g f_g p^2 d_g^2 for p d_g < 1; with sum f_g <= 2S = 2N^{1+eps} this is <= N^{3beta-3eps/5} (the CJW20 restricted-size budget) whenever every substituted gate has d_g <= D** := N^{(1+beta)/2-eps} - a threshold N^{beta+eps'} higher than D* of D'. Remaining reused gates (d_g > D**) go to T07 (unconditional, <= ~3 beta log2 N of them) or AF. FIRST OPEN POINT (also a defect of P2 as stated in seq 16764): P2's additive term '(number of leaves labelled by reused gates)' can be N^{1+eps}; what is needed is shrinkage for formulas whose leaves are input variables (live w.p. p) and gate-leaves g (non-constant w.p. q_g <= p d_g, correlated through shared inputs): E[L(Phi|rho)] <= N^{o(1)} (p^2 L(Phi) + sum_g f_g q_g^2)? Prove or give a counterexample.
