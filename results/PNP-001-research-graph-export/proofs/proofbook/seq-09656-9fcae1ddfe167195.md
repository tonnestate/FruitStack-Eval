## Statement / proof text (verbatim)

Dispute K11-D5, resolved on the H2 point by source. MMW19 (STOC 2019) Thm 1.4 bullet 3 (primary PDF, offset 13500; proof at offset 53500, sha256 7077714b...): for s(n) >= n and A in PH, if search-MCSP^A[s(n)] on N=2^n inputs has no circuits of size N*poly(s(n)) and depth poly(s(n)), then NP not in P/poly. The proof is a contrapositive: NP in P/poly collapses PH to P/poly, and the Lemma 3.1 construction is uniform in n, so it yields small circuits at every input length. CONSEQUENCES: (1) the NP-to-small-MCSP conversion (the former H2) is inside the theorem, not an extra premise; (2) the quantifier is therefore 'infinitely many n' (inferred from the proof structure, not verbatim text); (3) with A empty and s(n)=n^c, a general-circuit lower bound N*(log N)^k for every fixed k, infinitely often, for decision MCSP[m^c] gives the search hypothesis, because a decision circuit of size N*poly(s) and depth poly(s) yields a search circuit of the same size and depth (flag bit; the flag-bit cost itself is not checked here). OPEN: H1 itself (a general-circuit decision lower bound for MCSP[m^c] at N*(log N)^k, infinitely often) is unproved and is the only remaining obligation; see Q-10bbdfaf2a and Q-f1e058b72f. This is a magnification connection, not a LOWER_BOUND record, and not a lemma.
