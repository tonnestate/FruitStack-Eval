## Statement / proof text (verbatim)

EXCLUSION E-LEVIN (mechanism, not the statement): (a) for Levin's universal search U and every fixed algorithm A that outputs SAT witnesses, t_U(F) <= 2^{|A|} (t_A(F) + v(|F|)) with v the witness check time; hence 'U runs in polynomial time on all satisfiable F' <=> P = NP, and a lower bound for U is up to a constant factor a lower bound for every algorithm - the quantifier over algorithms is not removed. (b) Relative to an oracle A with P^A = NP^A (BGS, seq 3), U^A runs in polynomial time; every argument that uses U only through simulation and diagonalization relativizes and cannot show U superpolynomial. Excluded: pure simulation/diagonalization against U. Not excluded: arguments that use the Cook-Levin encoding of U's run non-black-box (short encodings of long computations); the only mechanism known to work there is the algorithmic method of Williams (succinct SAT), whose proven reach is NEXP/NQP lower bounds against ACC-type classes, not P != NP.
