## Statement / proof text (verbatim)

For every positive 1-in-3 instance: a family of 3-element clauses C_1..C_m over variables {1..n}. There exists v in GF(3)^n taking at most two distinct values with sum_{j in C_i} v_j = 1 (mod 3) for all i if and only if there exists u in {0,1}^n with |u intersect C_i| = 1 for all i. Proof sketch: A*1 = 0 mod 3; v = a*1 + (b-a)*u gives Av = (b-a)Au; Au in {1,2} gives a 1-in-3 solution either directly or via complement 1-u.
