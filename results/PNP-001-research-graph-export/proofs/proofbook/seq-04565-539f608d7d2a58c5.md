## Statement / proof text (verbatim)

For positive 1-in-3 formulas with every variable in at most 2 clauses, satisfiability equals the existence of a matching covering all clauses in the graph whose vertices are clauses, whose edges are variables in exactly 2 clauses, and where each variable in exactly 1 clause is a pendant half-edge. Consequently this class is decidable in polynomial time (Edmonds), and the n=6..10 census (A0663) counts non-perfect-matchable formulas rather than testing an open question.
