## Statement / proof text (verbatim)

LEVEL 1a monotonicity lemma. Let h(y) = SAT(F(y)), where each triple T is an input x_T that is a constant 0/1 or a single hard-input bit y_j (no negation), and F(y) = {T : x_T = 1}. Then h is monotone decreasing in y, because F(y) is monotone in y and SAT is a down-set in the triple family: if F(y') is satisfiable and F(y) is a subfamily of F(y'), then F(y) is satisfiable. Consequences: (i) MOD_q for every q >= 2 is neither monotone nor antitone, so it is not a negation-free projection of SAT or of NOT-SAT; (ii) MAJ is monotone increasing, so NOT-SAT (monotone increasing) does not exclude it by this test; (iii) the test applies to negation-free projections only, not to AC0 reductions, which may compute non-monotone gadgets before feeding SAT.
