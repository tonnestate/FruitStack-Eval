## Statement / proof text (verbatim)

VERIFIED ALGEBRAIC TRANSFER, steps 4-6 (owner contract 2026-11-27; PI reading of both papers; CLAIMED, Claude family). EXTERNAL INPUTS, UNVERIFIED, kept separate from Proofbook results: OpenAI 'VP and VNP are different' (openai/math 3344da5, Lean OAI.Computability.VPVNP.Standalone.vp_ne_vnp) and Anthropic 'VP != VNP over every field' (anthropics/formal-math 11b6c7e, vp-vnp, Lean Solution.mainTheorem etc.). Kernel build, #print axioms and Comparator are running in a bwrap sandbox; results follow in a separate row. STEP 4, mechanism. Both proofs trade tensor rigidity against diagonalization. Anthropic: Thm 2.2 (thm_alt) gives a flat or a contracting case. Flat case: lem_balanced splits the polynomial at every gate, so the output coefficient tensor is a sum of terms of rank <= L across balanced sets; Euclidean prices force log L >= l k^{3/2}/16 for f_{k,l}. Contracting case: thm_cut, with no circuit, cuts every weighted nondeterministic computation into short pieces; lem_lowdeg turns a circuit of size v^a into an accelerator, a program whose circuit constants act only as multiplicative named weights and whose runs take v^{H-16} < v^H steps; lem_postpone and prop_next amplify; thm_noacc gives V = 1 + V, impossible. OpenAI: Euclidean tensor pricing (exists_balanced_cutFactors, see 17563) plus replay diagonalization against the counting family f_n, whose c_x counts accepting paths of a universal machine. In both proofs the circuit enters only at the C-steps (formal multilinear block part or formal low-degree part); the hard families enter only through clause sums. STEP 5, translation. By 17584 over the semiring F[Y] with weights set to variables, every clause sum is a p-projection of U_G for a polynomial-size pinned graph G: boolean vertices carry the bits, AND gadgets give indicator vertices for local assignments, and their colour-1 variables are replaced by the local weights. f_{k,l}, Phi and f_n are clause sums, so each is a projection of a U_G family. This is a corollary of 17584 and not new; Valiant/Buergisser parsimonious completeness is standard (from memory). Consequence, conditional on the external results: the U_G families are not in VP. That is a consequence, not a premise. STEP 6, P = NP against intermediate theorems; result negative and exact. (a) C-steps. A polynomial-time SAT algorithm yields Boolean circuits, that is F2-circuits for some g = f + sum_i (x_i^2 - x_i) h_i. lem_balanced and lem_lowdeg read the multilinear block part and the low-degree part of the formal output, and for such g these are unrelated to f. Counterexamples: Anthropic Sec. 2.5, where (x11^2 - x11) x21...xk1 vanishes on {0,1} but has nonzero block-linear part; 17571, IP_n with a linear-size circuit and full cut rank. So the hypotheses of the C-steps are not met. (b) Diagonal step. thm_noacc refutes accelerators. A uniform algorithm from P = NP, or even from P^#P = P, is an accelerator only if it computes the weighted sum of tau-plain instances, size v^{H-32}, in v^{H-16} steps. Collapse simulations raise the time exponent by the factor c of the assumed algorithm and never lower it below the plain-run bound, so no accelerator arises. A nonuniform Boolean circuit branches on its advice; that is excluded by the model, because names may only be multiplied in (Anthropic 2.5: 'no way to run it faster than gate by gate'). Hence no intermediate theorem of either proof is contradicted by P = NP along these derivations. The exact obstruction: formal polynomial structure (C-steps) and exponent-lowering speedup in the weighted-sum model (diagonal step). No new lower-bound premise and no conditional chain; no obligation closed.
