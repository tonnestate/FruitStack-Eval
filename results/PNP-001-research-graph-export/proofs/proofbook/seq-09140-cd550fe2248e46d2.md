## Statement / proof text (verbatim)

For the natural adder-plus-comparator encoding (L-b1), the first parameter of a THR o THR instance to violate the hypothesis of the OpenAI counting theorem (<= c n equations, constant degree, <= polylog monomials) is the EQUATION COUNT, not the variable count, the degree (<= 2) or the monomial count (<= 4). Per bottom gate there are Theta(n log W) equations, so m bottom gates give Theta(m n log W) equations; with m = n^1.5 and W >= 2 this is n^2.5 log W >> c n.
