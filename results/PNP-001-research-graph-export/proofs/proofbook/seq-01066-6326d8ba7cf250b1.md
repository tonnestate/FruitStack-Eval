## Statement / proof text (verbatim)

For positive 1-in-3 with equalities A x = 1: a depth-1 Chvatal-Gomory refutation exists iff A x = 1 has no integer solution; with multipliers in (1/3)Z iff GF(3)-inconsistent; with (1/2)Z never. Computed: all 900 minimal GF(3) gap cores (n=6) are integer-solvable, 200/200 random GF(3)-inconsistent sets are not. So the gap cores are exactly the UNSAT instances surviving depth-1 CG. Correction: D-016h's 'depth-1 never refutes' was restricted to half-integer multipliers.
