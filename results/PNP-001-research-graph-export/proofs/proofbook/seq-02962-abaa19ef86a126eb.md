## Statement / proof text (verbatim)

For every finite loopless cubic multigraph G, let Phi(G) be the positive 1-in-3 formula with one variable per edge of G and one clause per vertex listing its three incident edge variables. Then every variable of Phi(G) occurs in exactly 2 clauses, and Phi(G) is satisfiable if and only if G has a perfect matching. Proof: a satisfying assignment marks a set of edges such that each vertex has exactly one marked incident edge, which is the definition of a perfect matching (loopless, so no edge counts twice at a vertex).
