## Statement / proof text (verbatim)

Degeneration stability of dual and conormal data. Theorem C': the rank-<=1 locus (dual of det_n) has a Groebner degeneration (flat limit of torus translates, Hilbert function preserved) to C(2n-2,n-1) coordinate (2n-2)-planes; hence every variety spanning <= 2n-2 dimensions lies in a flat limit of GL-translates of it, and no obstruction using only containment of the dual of a multiplicity-one component of z^{n-m}per_m in such a limit can exclude n >= (m^2+1)/2 (Betti numbers/regularity are not containment-monotone and cannot be used at all). Lemma D'': along a curve in GL.det_n to f_0, the conormal of a multiplicity-one component of V(f_0) is an irreducible COMPONENT of the flat limit of conormals (all hypersurface conormals have dimension N-2); consequences: slotwise polar-degree domination and the bigraded Hilbert function bound H_{Con V}(a,b) <= H_{Con det_n}(a,b). Proposition Q: at the level of cycle classes this is equivalent to polar-degree domination (conormals of linear spaces give all unit classes). The kernel incidence of the cubic preprint is the conormal correspondence of det_n. Open obligation O1: an invariant of components of flat limits of GL-translates of Con(det_n), not determined by the class, bounded for all such limits and violated by Con(cone over V(per_m)) at n = omega(m^3).
