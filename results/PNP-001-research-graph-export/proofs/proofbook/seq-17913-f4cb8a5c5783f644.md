## Statement / proof text (verbatim)

PRISM FACTORIZATION OF THE UNIVERSAL FUNCTION (contract 'Universal Prism Transformation', par. 3; CLAIMED, Claude family, no cross-family review). Definitions: U_G(X) = sum_{c in Col3(G)} prod_v X_{v,c(v)}. For H = H(G) (17911), U_H(X,Y,Z) uses X on the copies of V(G), Y_{i,j} = [colour of R_i is j] on the reference triangle, and Z on the gadget vertices. Statement: for every finite graph G, U_H(X,Y,1) = U_G(X) * perm(Y) with perm(Y) = sum_{pi in S3} prod_i Y_{i,pi(i)}. Moreover U_G(X) = coefficient of Y00 Y11 Y22 = U_H(X, I, 1). Proof: a proper colouring of H gives the triangle R a bijection pi. For fixed pi and fixed c in Col3(G), the gadget extension exists and is unique (17911 (a)). Hence the restriction to V(G) gives a bijection from the fibre over pi onto Col3(G). The sets are equal, and pi-invariance of Col3(G) is not needed for that. Summing over pi with Z = 1 gives the product. Setting Y = I kills every pi except the identity. Assumption: G is attached to R only through the gadgets. If vertices of G are pinned to R, the fibres become pi-dependent. Example: pinned path P3, Col not invariant under S3, checked. Validation: fibres over all 6 pi equal Col3(G) with multiplicity 1 for K3, P3, C5, C4+chord and the triangular prism; K4 gives 0 = 0 (scratchpad/prism/uh.py). Char 3: perm(J) = 6, so U_H(X,1,1) = 6 U_G = 0 in char 2 and 3. The full count vanishes, while the coefficient extraction Y = I is exact in every characteristic. Complexity consequence: U_G = U_H(X,I,1) and U_H(X,Y,1) = U_G * perm_3(Y) (a constant-size factor) are mutual p-projections. The prism transformation is therefore a p-equivalence of the universal functions. It neither gains nor loses algebraic complexity; this matches the contract warning that kernel preservation is not complexity preservation. The lattice index [L_H:M_H] = 3^r (17911) is a property of the colouring set, not of the circuit complexity of U_H. No obligation closed.
