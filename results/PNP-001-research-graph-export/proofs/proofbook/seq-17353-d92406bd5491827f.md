## Statement / proof text (verbatim)

KERNEL-CUT-RANK (owner derivation 2026-11-26, director check; CLAIMED). K_G over F_q (q = 3): x_v in F_q^3 with sum 0, z_{e,i} = -x_{u,i} - x_{v,i}, dim K_G = 2|V|; T_G = indicator of K_G. VERIFIED BY AUTHOR: (i) for every linear K and coordinate split L|R the flattening of 1_K is a disjoint union of all-ones blocks indexed by K/(K_L + K_R), so its rank is q^{dim K - dim K_L - dim K_R} over every coefficient field (this is the matroid connectivity lambda_K(L) and the minimal-trellis state-space dimension of a linear code at a cut, classical: Forney, Muder); (ii) for a vertex split U|U' and any placement of the edge coordinates, every crossing edge forces one endpoint to lose both degrees of freedom, also when the three z_{e,i} are split between the sides (then one endpoint receives >= 2 coordinates); the endpoints losing 2 form a vertex cover, the optimum is a minimum cover, and Koenig gives min rank = q^{2 nu(G[U,U'])} (= 9^nu); (iii) bounded-degree expanders give 2^{Omega(|V|)} for every balanced vertex split. STRENGTH: membership in K_G is a linear system (3|E| mod-3 checks, O(|E|) gates; one-pass in a good order with about lambda log q bits), so the cut rank is exactly tight for cut-respecting models (deterministic communication across that cut, fixed-order ROBP/OBDD width, fixed-order streaming space) and gives nothing for general circuits; consistent with the open question of seq 8834 ('a large invariant alone cannot imply hardness'). For varying-cut sums (Raz-type random-partition arguments) the rank q^{2 nu} is a constant fraction of the exponent below the maximal flattening rank of a balanced coordinate split, while such arguments need near-maximal rank; and 1_K = prod_j (1 - l_j^2) is a product of 3|E| quadratics. Consequence: any hardness must come from the Boolean Exact-One restriction (K_G intersected with the Exact-One set), not from the kernel; that intersection is the object the next step must treat. Width theorem w* = 2 tw(G)+1 itself: Lean-checked (seq 4058, 4079), novelty question open (seq 8795). Not a gap closure.
