## Statement / proof text (verbatim)

INTERVAL-LINEARISATION (owner 2026-11-27, director check; CLAIMED, needs review by another model family). Setting: full roABP-IPS refutation C(x,y) of Psi_n (axioms = clause products Q_j of literal indicators, placeholders y_j), width w in a total order pi of x and y; I_j = span in pi of {y_j} union vars(Q_j); N = placeholders occurring in a monomial with another placeholder or with degree >= 2; p_N = max over cuts of #{j in N : I_j crosses the cut} (p_N <= owner's p). CLAIM: Psi_n has an roABP-IPS_LIN refutation (functional form, Boolean-axiom terms free) of width <= 1 + m w 2^{p_N} in the induced x-order; with Theorem 12 (seq 5128): log2 w + p_N >= c1 n - O(log m), c1 = Theorem 12 exponent constant (not explicit). PROOF. (A) Telescoping: C(x,Q) - C(x,0) = sum_j [C(Q_<j,1,0_>j) - C(Q_<j,0,0_>j)] Q_j =: sum_j Q_j D_j pointwise on the cube, since Q_j in {0,1} there; so sum_j Q_j D_j = 1 on the cube. For j not in N, D_j = coefficient g_j(x) of y_j, width <= w (coefficient extraction, as Lemma 15 (i), seq 5174). For j in N only placeholders in N are substituted. (B) Cut rank: at a pi-cut write C = sum_{k<=w} A_k(x_L,y_L) B_k(x_R,y_R). For y_i on the left with I_i crossing, Q_i = L_i(x_L) R_i(x_R) (on the cube); Moebius expansion in the Boolean values gives A_k(x_L,Q) = sum_{T subset S_L} alpha_{k,T}(x_L) prod_{i in T} L_i prod_{i in T} R_i, each term rank 1; same for B_k. Non-crossing placeholders are one-sided functions (no cost). Hence cube-evaluation rank of D_j at the cut <= w 2^{p_N}. ml(D_j) has coefficient-matrix rank = cube-evaluation rank (Kronecker product of invertible 2x2 transforms, every field), so ml(D_j) is an roABP of width <= w 2^{p_N} in the induced order (Nisan). (C) sum_j Q_j ml(D_j) - 1 vanishes on the cube, hence lies in the Boolean ideal. Theorem 12 is functional: Lemma T (report l.268-274) uses only 'on the cube the Boolean-axiom terms vanish' and the cube rank of a_D at a cut, so the Boolean quotients h need no width bound. REVIEW POINT: check that Lemma O (order reduction, report l.323-346) is likewise only a restriction of the a_D (functional), so Theorem 12 applies to cube-rank widths. STRENGTH/REDUNDANCY: incomparable with Theorem 14 (placeholder degree <= c' n, seq 5169) and Lemma 15 (t placeholder monomials, seq 5174); covers part of the Theorem 14 remainder (degree > c' n with nonlinear placeholders spread so few cross any cut). Not covered: certificates with >= c1 n - log(mw) nonlinear placeholders crossing some cut; Theorem 16 (seq 5192) shows every order has a cut crossed by K = Omega(n) disjoint clauses, so the canonical product certificate has p_N = Omega(n) and the bound is vacuous there (Theorem 16 handles it). Whether c1 exceeds the minimal crossing density is not decided (c1 not explicit). Telescoping is routine (TR26-070 Sec. 7, local paper.txt l.727-753; Grochow-Pitassi Prop. 2.1); novelty of the 2^p substitution cost not checked in the literature. Restricted proof-system lower bound only; no consequence for P != NP; M9 remains open.
