(3) Algebraic descent (LabelEvaluator.Model): one prime p (equal to q, or with poly(n) bits in characteristic 0) and a finite slice of the zero locus with bounded point count and a normalized linear functional on its coordinate algebra. The value read back depends only on the equations, not on which circuit solved them. Compression and replay make the counting fit the clock n^{10^9}; that part was not verified. FORMAL VS BOOLEAN: the equation rows, U_n, the machine D and the read-back are Boolean or counting semantics. Uses of the formal identity in proof order: (a) wire_expanders_of_small_circuits inside fixed_templates_of_small_circuits (geometric hinge: small circuits for P n give a wire expander or shortening; not analysed further); (b) exists_compressorSolution_of_circuit, which needs hpoly : circuit.polynomial = P n and produces graded parse labels up to degree n (CircuitGrammar.lean: arbitrary constants, no homogeneous intermediate gates required). FIRST NON-TRANSFERABLE OPERATION (proved obstruction for (b)): a Boolean analogue, 'every Boolean circuit of size s for f:{0,1}^n -> F_2 yields a degree-n graded grammar with poly(s) labels whose indicator evaluations equal f', would imply P/poly subset NC^2/poly. Proof sketch: a grammar with poly labels and degree n evaluates as a homogeneous arithmetic circuit of size poly(s) (the matrix-path identity of LabelEvaluator; this premise is not independently checked); VSBR/MRK depth reduction gives fan-in-2 depth O(log n log s + log^2 s); simulate F_2 gates by Boolean gates at indicator inputs. P/poly subset NC^2/poly is open and believed false, so step (b) is the first operation that does not transfer without proving an open collapse. Step (a) is earlier in proof order but not analysed, so (b) is the first operation proven non-transferable. TRANSFERABLE PART (PI derivation): U_n(x) = #Sol(Phi_x) for a positive Exact-One formula. Take the tableau of the clocked machine, record the branch choice per step (rows have 0, 1 or 2 branches) and pad with deterministic idle steps after halting, so accepting paths correspond bijectively to tableaux. Use the Cook-Levin local cell constraints, made parsimonious and positive via T41 (constant 0 from E(T,a,b), E(T,b,c), E(T,c,a); long clauses split as in 17540). Input selectors: EO(y_{i,0}, y_{i,1}) with weight X_{i,b} on y_{i,b}. Then P n = generating polynomial of one Exact-One family Psi_n restricted to selector weights, with size O(T^2), T = n^{10^9}. Like Z_{G,q} (17540), P n is a projection of an Exact-One generating polynomial. Graph-colouring gadgets are not parsimonious (colour symmetries and gadget multiplicities); not established. STRENGTH: no Boolean lower bound follows; no obligation closed; the VP != VNP claim itself remains an unreviewed external claim that was not built here.
