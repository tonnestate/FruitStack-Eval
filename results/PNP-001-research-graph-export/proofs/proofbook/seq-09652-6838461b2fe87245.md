## Statement / proof text (verbatim)

Dispute K11-D5, verified source step. MMW19 (STOC 2019) Thm 1.4, third bullet, read from the primary PDF (people.csail.mit.edu/rrw/MCSP-MKTP-stoc19.pdf, offset 13500, sha256 7077714b...): for s(n) >= n and A in PH, if search-MCSP^A[s(n)] on inputs of length N=2^n does not have circuits of N*poly(s(n)) size and poly(s(n)) depth, then NP not in P/poly. CONSEQUENCES: (a) with A = empty oracle (in PH) and s(n)=n^c, a lower bound for DECISION MCSP[m^c] against general circuits of size N*(log N)^k (any depth) gives the search hypothesis, because a search solver's flag bit is a decision circuit of the same size and depth; (b) therefore no separate H2 premise is needed, and NP-in-P/poly-to-small-MCSP-circuits is carried by the theorem. OPEN: (i) the quantifier in Thm 1.4 ('for all n' vs 'infinitely many n') is not yet read; (ii) H1 itself, a general-circuit lower bound N*polylog(N) for decision MCSP[m^c], is unproved and is the real remaining obligation (see Q-f1e058b72f).
