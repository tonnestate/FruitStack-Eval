## Statement / proof text (verbatim)

D4 REUSE-TO-CORRELATION DECOMPOSITION (exact): let C be a De Morgan circuit with gamma gates g_1..g_gamma of fan-out > 1 and S gates. For a in {0,1}^gamma let F_a(x) = AND_j [P_j(x,a) = a_j] AND P_out(x,a), where P_j, P_out are the principal formulas (CHOPRS20 Thm 30) with reused gates replaced by constants a. Then L(F_a) <= S+gamma, the F_a have pairwise disjoint supports and C = OR_a F_a. For any two distributions Y, Z: Pr_Y[C]-Pr_Z[C] = sum_a (Pr_Y[F_a]-Pr_Z[F_a]), so some formula F_a of size <= S+gamma has advantage >= Adv(C)/2^gamma. Reuse costs a factor 2^gamma in CORRELATION, not in size (T07 pays it in size).
