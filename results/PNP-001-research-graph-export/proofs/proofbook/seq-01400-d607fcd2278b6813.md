## Statement / proof text (verbatim)

Blindness criterion (from D-018 + j in kernel): for a GF(3)-consistent positive 1-in-3 formula with solution set y+K, K = <j, k_1..k_{d-1}>, the formula is UNSAT iff every vector y + sum b_i k_i (b in GF(3)^{d-1}) takes all three values of GF(3). In particular at nullity 2: UNSAT iff y, y+k, y+2k all take three values. Hence 1-in-3-SAT is equivalent to: does an affine GF(3)-subspace (given by A x = 1) contain a vector with at most two distinct values (up to shift: a 0/1 vector) - degree-1 GF(3) reasoning cannot decide it. Heuristic (random model, NOT proved): blindness becomes typical when d < ~0.37 n (3^(d-1) * 3 (2/3)^n << 1).
