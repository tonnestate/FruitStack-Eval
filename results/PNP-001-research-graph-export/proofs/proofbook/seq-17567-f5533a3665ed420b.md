## Statement / proof text (verbatim)

For gate elimination on MCSP[m^c] with c>=1, the zero-restriction mechanism from Wittcop's 2N bound (which works for threshold m-1) fails because: (1) at threshold m^c, the radius of accepted tables around small circuits grows; (2) the small-support obstruction (t <= m^(c-1) ones implies YES) means many pairs at large Hamming distance are both YES; (3) the spectral/Hoffman step depends on the threshold being m-1. Need a NEW restriction invariant that survives at polynomial threshold.
