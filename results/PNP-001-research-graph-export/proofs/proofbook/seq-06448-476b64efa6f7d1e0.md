## Statement / proof text (verbatim)

FORMULA ANALOGUE OF THE LOFF-SHERIF-TALEBANFARD-UGAZIO SELF-REFERENTIAL BARRIER (CONJECTURE, not derived; source: PNP-001-LIT-8b-2026-11-21.md page 2). LSTU Thm 3: the Trevisan-Xue generator is localized in constant depth d (pseudorandom truth table = AC0 circuit of depth d, size 2^{n^{O(1/d)}}), and its security uses a derandomized switching lemma; consequence: a poly-size constant-depth natural property cannot prove 2^{n^eps} lower bounds for depth-d circuits unless eps < 7/(d-5). Formula analogue: replace AC0 by De Morgan formulas of size N^beta and the switching lemma by Hastad shrinkage (random restriction keeping each variable with probability p maps a size-s formula to size about p^2 s). Needed ingredients: (a) a local generator with seed N^{o(1)} whose outputs are De Morgan-formula truth tables of size poly(N); (b) a security proof against size-s formulas using shrinkage at rate p; (c) a natural property whose proof of L(MCSP[N^beta]) >= N^a yields a distinguisher in the shrinkage-secured class. FAILING STEP: (a)/(b) require a depth parameter that makes the localization argument work; shrinkage has no depth parameter, so the LSTU localization step is not available from the sources read. Step (c) is not checkable without the CJW20 proof method (absent from PNP-001-LIT-4, which gives only the Thm 1.6 statement).
