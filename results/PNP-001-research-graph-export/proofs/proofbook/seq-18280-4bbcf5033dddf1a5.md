## Statement / proof text (verbatim)

CORRECTION OF THE GRAPH-POLYNOMIAL DEFINITIONS (pinned / unpinned / fully weighted). Let G contain the reference triangle R0,R1,R2. PINNED polynomial P_j(G;y)=sum over proper 3-colourings c with c(R_i)=i of prod_v y_v^{[c(v)=j]} (17584 uses j=1). UNPINNED colour-1 polynomial U(G;y)=sum over ALL proper colourings of prod_v y_v^{[c(v)=1]}. Every c is uniquely sigma^-1 o c' with c' pinned, hence U = sum_{sigma in S3} P_{sigma(1)} = 2(P_0+P_1+P_2). For any graph with an edge the involution swapping colours 0 and 2 is a fixed-point-free bijection on proper colourings preserving every monomial of U, so U is divisible by 2 and vanishes identically in characteristic 2: the factor 1+e_v1(y_v-1) does not distinguish colours 0,2 and is only admissible for PINNED graphs. FULLY WEIGHTED polynomial W(G;X)=sum over proper colourings prod_v prod_{c=0..2}(1-e_vc+e_vc X_vc) = sum_c prod_v X_{v,c(v)} (one-hot e). Setting X_{R_i,c}=[c=i] (0/1 constants) kills all sigma != id: W|_{X_R=delta} = P(G;X) pinned, a zero-one projection. VNP: W and P are sums over e in {0,1}^{3|V|} of g=prod_v oh_v(e) * prod_{uv}(1-sum_c e_uc e_vc) * prod_v prod_c(1-e_vc+e_vc X_vc); the edge factor is 0/1-valued ONLY on one-hot e, and oh_v (=1 exactly on one-hot vectors, 0 elsewhere on {0,1}^3) kills all other e, so g is 0/1 times a weight monomial; deg g and size of g are O(|V|+|E|); W,P in VNP over every field. HC_h = rho(P) hence W,P are VNP-hard (HC VNP-complete over every field: OpenAI hamperm Sec.1.1, EXTERNAL); the equivalence 'in VP iff VP=VNP' holds for P (pinned) and W (fully weighted), and is FALSE-as-stated for the unpinned colour-1 polynomial U in characteristic 2 (identically 0).
