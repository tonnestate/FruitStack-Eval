## Statement / proof text (verbatim)

Physical NP solvers and their hidden costs: linear optics driven by classical (coherent) light is classically simulable in time polynomial in the number of modes; optical 'solvers' for subset sum / Hamiltonian path move the exponential into the number of paths, delays or intensity (Oltean et al. 2008-2009); coherent Ising machines minimise QUBO energies heuristically without polynomial guarantees; reading an amplitude of size 2^-n needs exponentially many repetitions or exponential precision; PostBQP = PP (Aaronson 2005) shows postselection is such a hidden resource; Valiant-Vazirani: SAT reduces (randomised) to instances with at most one solution, so a solver cannot avoid the C = 1 case.
