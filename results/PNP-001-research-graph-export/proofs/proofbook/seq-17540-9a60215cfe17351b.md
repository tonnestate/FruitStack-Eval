## Statement / proof text (verbatim)

STATE-SUM INTEGRATION (owner 2026-11-27). SOURCE: 'Paths, trees and grids separate Valiant's hierarchy', OpenAI manuscript in the public repo openai/math, dated 2026-11-24, not peer-reviewed; per the repo's Lean doc the separations are Lean-checked for degree-component border circuits, and the explicit n^{Omega((log n)^{1/12})} grid bound is not formalised. Definition (paper eq. 1.1): Z_{G,q}(X) = sum_{a in [q]^E} prod_v X_{v, a|E(v)}, with an independent table at each vertex. IDENTITY (PI proof, statesum_check.py: path q=3, triangle, multi-edge, C4 equal as monomial multisets): Phi_{G,q} on positive exact-one clauses: per edge EO(y_{e,1..q}); per vertex EO(s_{v,alpha} : alpha in [q]^{E(v)}); for e in E(v), i in [q]: EO({y_{e,j}: j != i} u {s_{v,alpha}: alpha_e = i}), equivalent to y_{e,i} = sum_{alpha_e = i} s_{v,alpha}. The solutions are in bijection with a in [q]^E. Long clauses go to 3-ary positive exact-one parsimoniously: EO(x1,x2,z), EO(z,z',F), EO(z',x3,...), with z, z' determined and F the constant 0 from the T41 gadget E(T,a,b), E(T,b,c), E(T,c,a) (unique solution T=1, a=b=c=0). With weight X_{v,alpha} on s_{v,alpha} and 1 on every other variable, P_Phi(X) = sum_{y in Sol(Phi)} prod_{s_{v,alpha}=1} X_{v,alpha} = Z_{G,q}(X). So Z is a projection of the generic generating polynomial of an Exact-One family, and any arithmetic lower bound for Z transfers to it. ARITHMETIC vs BOOLEAN: the transfer stops at arithmetic circuits computing the polynomial formally. COUNTEREXAMPLE to any 'arithmetic generating-polynomial bound => Boolean decision bound' rule: bipartite perfect matching is decidable in P, yet its generating polynomial is the permanent (VNP-complete; the companion manuscript claims superpolynomial bounds for it). Known implications run the other way (Buergisser: NP not in P/poly plus GRH => VP != VNP over C, from memory, verify). No Boolean lower bound, no closure of OBL-R2/R6/R7/R8/ST-ABS/Q-C, and no P != NP progress follows, even if the manuscript is correct.
