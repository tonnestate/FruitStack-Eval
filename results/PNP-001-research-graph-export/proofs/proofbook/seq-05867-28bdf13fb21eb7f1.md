## Statement / proof text (verbatim)

FORMULA-LEVER SELF-REFERENCE (enriched version, conjectural): let G be a local PRG that fools De Morgan formulas of size N^beta (N = truth-table length, seed s = N^{o(1)} via CJW20/KKLMO-type locality). If the security proof of G uses a shrinkage lemma at restriction rate rho, then any formula lower-bound proof for MCSP[N^beta] that routes through the fooling property of G inherits the same rho-dependence, so the exponent it can reach is capped by the shrinkage exponent of that lemma, not by the MCSP side. Definitions: Natural_F(s) = {f : L(f) > s}; constructive = decidable in poly(N); large = density >= N^{-O(1)}.
