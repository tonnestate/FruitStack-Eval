## Statement / proof text (verbatim)

Valiant's depth-reduction lemma (primary form: remove r*m/log2(d) edges to reach depth d/2^r) implies that reducing a circuit of size m = Θ(n) and depth d = N^γ to depth N^γ' requires removing a fraction (γ-γ')/γ of all wires. This is feasible (fraction < 1) iff γ' > 0. Reducing polylog depth to O(1) requires removing all wires (fraction → 1), hence is impossible. The spec's form (k*m/log2(d), depth d/2^(k-1)) with spec's k = r+1 is an equivalent restatement.
