## Statement / proof text (verbatim)

IMPROVED HAMILTONIAN-CYCLE GRAPH G_h with |V|=O(h^2 log h) (replaces the O(h^4 log h) table construction of T58). Per vertex v in [h] a position p_v in {0..h-1}, m=ceil(log2 h) free Boolean bits; p_1=0 (force0), range p_v<h (force1 on a comparator), pairwise distinct (for a<b: EQ(p_a,p_b) forced 0), inc_a=p_a+1 mod h computed ONCE per vertex (ripple half-adder, O(m) gates, wrap mask O(m)), s_ab=EQ(inc_a,p_b) for every ordered pair a!=b, weight X_ab on colour 1 of the Boolean vertex s_ab. Gates: NOT, AND (corrected gadget), XNOR; EQ of m-bit words = m XNOR + (m-1) AND = O(m). Count: 1.5h(h-1) EQ gadgets of <=66m vertices each plus O(hm) per-vertex gadgets: |V(G_h)| <= 100 h^2 ceil(log2 h) + O(h log h) (exact bound computed: |V|/(h^2 m) = 103 at h=8, 93.5 at h=64, 95 at h=4096). PINNED polynomial of G_h with weights ONLY on the h(h-1) vertices s_ab: every valid assignment is a bijection p with p_1=0, s_ab=1 iff b follows a, exactly h of the s_ab equal 1, so the product is prod over the tour 1->...->1 of X_{ab}: P(G_h;(y_ab)) = sum over bijections with p_1=0 prod_{a!=b}(1-s_ab+s_ab y_ab) = HC_h(y) EXACTLY (each directed Hamiltonian tour once, positions run along the tour from vertex 1; homogeneous of degree h). Logic checked by exhaustive enumeration of bit assignments h=3..7 (monomial multiset = (h-1)! tours). Correctness of the colouring graph: every gadget has the unique-extension property (a gadget with Boolean inputs I and output O has exactly one colouring of its internal vertices and O for every input in {0,1}^I, with O=f(I); a constraint gadget has exactly one or zero). Acyclic composition: gadgets share only input/output vertices, their other vertices are pairwise non-adjacent, edges touch only own vertices and R; by induction on a topological order the colour of every gate output is determined by the free position bits, so proper pinned colourings of G_h are in bijection with assignments of the free bits satisfying all force constraints, with weight prod X_ab^{s_ab}. Characteristic independence: identity in N[y], hence in every commutative ring/field. No 6-fold colour multiplicity (R pinned). Fully weighted unpinned graph: size n=|V|, projection by 0/1 reference weights.
