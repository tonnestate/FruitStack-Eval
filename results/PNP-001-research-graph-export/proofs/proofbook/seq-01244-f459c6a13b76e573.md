## Statement / proof text (verbatim)

D-014f: Single-difference cap: if every nonzero entry A[u,v] of a Gal matrix has |v \ u| = 1, then rank(A) <= sum_j rank(A|R_j) <= k * max_j rank(A|R_j), so the Gal ratio is at most k = C(n,3). Proof (director): such an entry lies in exactly one clause rectangle R_j = {u: j not in u} x {v: j in v} (j = the unique element of v \ u), so the supports of the restrictions A|R_j are pairwise disjoint and A = sum_j A|R_j; rank is subadditive over a sum. Hence any improvement over k must use entries with |v \ u| >= 2 (where the rectangles overlap and subadditivity fails). Empirically consistent: cycle RC-8fd9326f, n=5 and n=6 over GF(2)/GF(3), 20 trials each, 0 cap violations, sum identity holds. 2026-11-18
