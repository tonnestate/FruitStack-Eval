## Statement / proof text (verbatim)

Meet-in-the-middle over the joint Boolean system (x,y) in {0,1}^(n+m) with Ax+By=c mod 3 decides satisfiability in time about 2^((n+m)/2) poly(n,m) independent of the rank r. This beats 2^n exactly when m < n. The kernel-enumeration saving 2^(n+m-r) beats 2^n exactly when r > m. Combined, a saving over 2^n for any representation needs m < n (MITM) or r > m (kernel).
