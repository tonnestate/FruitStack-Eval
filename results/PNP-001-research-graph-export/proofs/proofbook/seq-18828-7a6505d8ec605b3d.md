## Statement / proof text (verbatim)

T67 Theorem A (owner construction 2026-11-28 checked by the PI; CLAIMED, Claude family; theory/T67-counterexample-driven-observable-space-construction-2026-11-28.md). Given an equivalence oracle (SAT-search under H) for identities h = sum c_j f_j on X, the exact observable space V_g = span{g o F^k} is constructed with exactly d+1 oracle calls: keep reference states with invertible evaluation matrix E, solve E c = h(x_i), ask for a counterexample; a counterexample x* gives Schur complement s = h(x*) - sum c_j f_j(x*) != 0 so the extended matrix stays invertible; the span is Koopman-closed when every f_i o F is in it, hence equals V_g and Phi o F = M Phi, Phi(F^T x) = M^T Phi(x). Cost under H: (d+1)(|g|+d|F|+d^2)^kappa + O(d^3), evaluation O~(d^omega log T + d|F|). Finite checks (counter n<=7, LFSR n<=8, random permutations n<=8 over F_2): found r = true d, M exact, queries = d+1. Limits (unconditional accounting): the identity involves only poly-size circuits because d is the GLOBAL dimension (a single-trajectory version needs circuits containing F^t, exponential); general F^(2^n) is PSPACE-complete so H alone gives no existence of small structures; under H and T66 Thm 10.6 hierarchy-hard families have no small Sigma2-checkable structure. Not novel (multiplicity-automata learning with equivalence queries, Beimel et al. 2000, recalled). Open: ring version over Z/2^w (unit-pivot obstruction verified for g=2x), mixed rings, Sigma2 search of the output map, self-reference closure.
