## Statement / proof text (verbatim)

SEED-FLOOR (proof of the D5 claim, seq 16768): if a distribution G on {0,1}^k has support size <= M (e.g. seed length l, M = 2^l), then some AND of t = floor(log2 M) + 1 <= k literals has |Pr_G - Pr_U| >= 2^{-t}: of the 2^t patterns on the first t coordinates at most M < 2^t are hit, a missed pattern has Pr_G = 0 and Pr_U = 2^{-t}. Hence fooling De Morgan formulas of size >= t with error delta forces log2 M >= log2(1/delta) - 1. In the D4/AF mechanism (error 2^{-r-2} for r surviving reused gates): seed >= r + 1, and since YES outputs G(z) o rho have circuit size <= N^beta, the support on the star coordinates is <= 2^{O(N^beta log N)}, so r <= O(N^beta log N). Consequence: every PRG instantiation of AF has polylog(1/delta)-exponent a >= 1 and covers at most N^{beta+o(1)} surviving reused gates; circuits with more surviving reuse must first be reduced by D6'/NORM.
