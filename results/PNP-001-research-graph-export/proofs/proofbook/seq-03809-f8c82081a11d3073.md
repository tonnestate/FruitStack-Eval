## Statement / proof text (verbatim)

Degree transfer from 3-colouring: for a graph G let T(G) be the positive 1-in-3 formula with clauses (x_v1,x_v2,x_v3) for every vertex v and (x_uc,x_wc,y_uwc) with a fresh variable for every edge uw and colour c. (a) T(G) is GF(3)-consistent for every G. (b) T(G) is satisfiable iff G is 3-colourable. (c) Over GF(2) modulo x^2 = x, if the XOR/carry system of T(G) (L = 1+a+b+c, q = ab+ac+bc per clause) has a polynomial-calculus refutation of degree d, then the standard 3-colouring encoding of G (sum_c x_vc = 1, x_vc x_vc' = 0, x_uc x_wc = 0) has one of degree max(d,2): substitute y_uwc := 1 + x_uc + x_wc (affine, degree-preserving); edge L maps to 0, edge q to x_uc x_wc, y^2+y to 0, vertex L to the vertex axiom, vertex q to a sum of the axioms x_vc x_vc'. CONSEQUENCE if (c) holds: the linear PC-degree lower bounds for 3-colouring of bounded-degree graphs over every field (Lauria-Nordstrom 2017) give an explicit infinite family of GF(3)-blind positive 1-in-3 formulas with PC degree Omega(n) and, by Impagliazzo-Pudlak-Sgall, PC size exp(Omega(n)) in the XOR/carry encoding over GF(2) - a class result for PC/Groebner-based procedures, not P != NP.
