## Statement / proof text (verbatim)

ELEMENTARY IDENTITIES (verified independently; not cited, not claimed new). R=F2[x]/(x_i^2-x_i) is a Boolean ring (every element idempotent). D=prod(1-f_i) is the indicator of V(f), idempotent; E=1-D idempotent; DE=0; R=DR+ER; b_i=prod_{j<i}(1-f_j) gives b_i-b_{i+1}=b_i f_i, hence sum b_i f_i=b_1-b_{m+1}=E (telescoping); (f)=ER since E f_i=f_i and E in (f). Koszul d(e_i)=f_i, h=(sum b_i e_i)^ with a degree-1 element so dh+hd=d(eta)=sum b_i f_i=E and h^2=0. K=DK+EK as complexes: EK has identity null-homotopic hence acyclic; on DK the differential is 0 because D f_i=0 (factor (1-f_i)f_i=0), so H_k(K)=(DR)^{C(m,k)}. Checked with explicit F2 matrices on 60 random instances (n in {3,4}, m in 2..4): all identities, dh+hd=E in every degree, dim H_k=C(m,k)*|V(f)| (scratchpad/cert/kos.py).
