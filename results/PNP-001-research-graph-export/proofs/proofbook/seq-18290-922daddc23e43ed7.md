## Statement / proof text (verbatim)

GATE-IDEAL SATURATION THEOREM (T60 s1-2). Rees ring R=F_p[t,v]/(v^p-t^(p-1)v), rho:R->B[t] injective, t nonzerodivisor. For gate residuals r_j with ranges S_j, degree-d_j homogenizations H_j, J_C=(H_j), P_j=prod_{c in S_j\0}(t^(d_j)-H_j/c), P_C=prod P_j: H_jP_C=0; t^D-P_C in J_C (telescoping) with D=sum d_j k_j<=(p-1)sum d_j (F2 quadrics: 2s); hence J_C:t^inf = J_C:t^D = Ann(P_C) = rho^-1((G_1..G_s)B[t]) = homogeneous Rees ideal of the valid-computation graph. Machine-checked on a 3-gate F2 circuit (min k=1<=6) and on ternary Boolean-restricted variables (t^2-q^2=-H', H'q=0, q=y-2t). Exponent is an upper bound for the t-power only; gives poly-size circuit cofactors for the gate part but no poly-size IPS refutation (P_C*f=0 in R is the coNP-type identity).
