## Statement / proof text (verbatim)

CHARACTERIZATION OF THE COMPRESSION PREMISE; ROUTE CLOSED (owner 2026-11-27; PI check; CLAIMED, Claude family). U := there is a polynomial p such that for every transition circuit f of size m with s state bits and every j = O(s), the relation b = f^{2^j}(a) has an existential EO-graph representation of size <= p(m). Claim U <-> PSPACE subset of NP/poly. (=>) The PSPACE machine's configuration b is unique, so acceptance = exists b,y: G(start(x),b,y) and acc(b); this is NP/poly (17584 encoding). (<=) Iterated circuit value with binary j (f part of the input) is in PSPACE, hence has nondeterministic poly-size circuits; hardwire f and compile them to EO-graphs (17584). Under P=NP: U <-> P=PSPACE. (=>) PSPACE subset of P/poly, then PSPACE = MA (from memory) subset of Sigma2 = P; alternatively the constructive MinGraph of 17586. (<=) trivial. Verdict: the time-hierarchy route via compact configuration spaces is closed (17586). Relativization triage, unproven: Hall/support embeddings, semiring evaluation and coefficient conservation are oracle-free finite combinatorics; the only step that touches computation structure is the compilation of a circuit into G_C (Cook-Levin locality, arithmetization type, expected to algebrize per Aaronson-Wigderson, not proved here). Semantic minimization relativizes.
