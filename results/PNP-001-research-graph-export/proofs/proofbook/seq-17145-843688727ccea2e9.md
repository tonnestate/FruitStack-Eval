## Statement / proof text (verbatim)

T-NORM-F (refines seq 17140; CONDITIONAL on D4 seq 16760 and CORR-IK): the forest-size estimate s = p^2 N^{1+eps} is wrong in general. Tal's shrinkage E[L(F|rho)] = O(p^2 L log(1/p) + p sqrt(L)) applies per principal formula; with m principal formulas of sizes L_i (sum L_i = L <= N^{1+eps}, m up to N^{1+eps}), E[s] <= sum_i min(p L_i, O~(p^2 L_i + p sqrt(L_i))) <= O~(p^2 L + p sqrt(m L)), and circuits with all L_i = O(1) reach s ~ p L = N^{beta+6eps/5}. Worst case k^2/s >= p^2 N^2 / (2 p N^{1+eps}) = N^{beta-4eps/5}/2 (k = pN = N^{beta+eps/5}). Against D4's correlation >= 2^{-N^{beta-eta}} (w.p. >= 1 - N^{-eta} - 0.1 for gamma and s, Markov) the contradiction holds iff eta > 4eps/5 (+o(1)); with W budget N^{beta-2eta} >= 1 this needs 4eps/5 < eta < beta/2, i.e. eps < 5beta/8. The owner's conditions 1 - eps > beta - eta and beta + 6eps/5 < 1 used the formula-only size and are insufficient for general circuits.
