## Statement / proof text (verbatim)

For a positive 3-uniform clause set F with A its 0/1 incidence matrix (rhs 1), if F is GF(3)-consistent and unsatisfiable, then A x = 1 has no integer solution if and only if the Smith form of A contains a factor 9 in the position where the Smith form of [A|1] contains 3 (the gap is exactly one factor of 3, at the 3-part). Feasible blind sets never have a 9.
