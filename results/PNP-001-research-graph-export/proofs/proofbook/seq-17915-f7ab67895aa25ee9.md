## Statement / proof text (verbatim)

AFFINE RIGIDITY OF COLOURING SETS AND OF THEIR PROJECTIONS (owner theorem 2026-11-27 checked, PI extensions; CLAIMED, Claude family; novelty not checked). Scope note: USCCL is exactly min_B mu(H_B) = mu(G K_2). No broader conservation statement for parsimonious transformations is recorded. (R1, owner) If x, x+v, x+2v are proper 3-colourings over F3 and ij is an edge, then (x_i - x_j) + t(v_i - v_j) != 0 for t in F3, which forces v_i = v_j. Hence v is constant on every connected component and 0 on components with a pinned vertex. Proof checked; correct. (R2, PI) Exact cover number: let D be the span of the indicator vectors of the unpinned components (dim c_free). Every affine A = x + W contained in S = Col3(G, pins) has W in D, and x + D is contained in S. So the cosets of D partition S, and each A lies in one coset. Therefore a(S) = |S| / 3^{c_free}. For the pinned prism graph H(G) (connected, R pinned), a(Col3^pin(H)) = |Col3(G)| = U_G(1) over N. This is the owner's statement. Validation: no lines in pinned Col(H) for edgeless3, P3, K3, C5 and the prism graph (scratchpad/prism/rigid.py). (R3, PI) Projection rigidity: for T a subset of V and P = pi_T(Col3(G)), every point of P is a proper colouring of G[T]. R1 applies verbatim to G[T], so every line direction in P is a per-component shift of G[T]. Projection therefore never creates new line directions, and a(P) >= |P| / 3^{c(G[T])}. Validation: exhaustive over all T for C5, prism, W5 and three random 7-vertex graphs (13 edges); 0 non-shift directions found (scratchpad/prism/projlines.py). Example: projecting pinned Col(H) onto V(G) leaves Col3(G) with |Col3(G)|/3 shift lines for connected G (P3: 4, C5: 10, prism: 4); for edgeless G it gives F3^n with a = 1. (R4, PI) Two representations: in the positive representation (union of affine subspaces equal to S), existence elimination is projection, so a(pi S) <= a(S), which never grows; but the starting size is a(S) = |S| / 3^{c_free}, i.e. counting level. The negative representation of 17910 (union of affine subspaces equal to the complement) starts with m hyperplanes, and only projection can make it grow. The rigidity theorem constrains the positive representation only. The measured growth in 17910/17911 concerns the complement cover number, to which R1-R3 make no direct statement. Relevance: edgeless G already gives a = 3^n with trivial decision, so R1-R4 carry no hardness. No consequence of P=NP is derived. No obligation closed.
