CONTROL THEOREM K AND STATUS AFTER THE OWNER CHECK OF THEOREM H (CLAIMED; cross-family mission iteration 14 submitted). (1) Owner algebraic check of Theorem H step 4 (17916): agrees with the PI exact kernel computation. Gadget rows give C_0 = C_1 = C_2; the triangle gives A + B + C = 0, hence A_0 = A_1 = A_2; line sums give multiples of J. Steps 5 and 6 give divisibility inside L_G, not only coordinatewise. No assumption on G beyond unpinned and 3-colourable. (2) Theorem K (owner): let S be a nonempty affine subspace of F3^n of codimension r. Then acn(F3^n minus S) = 2r. Proof checked by PI. Translate and quotient by the direction space of S to reach F3^r minus {0}. The cover x_i = 1, x_i = 2 gives <= 2r. For the lower bound, every covering subspace avoids 0, so it lies in an affine hyperplane l_i = 1 with l_i linear (choose l_i zero on the direction space and 1 at a base point). Then P = prod (1 - l_i) is the indicator of 0. Its reduced form is prod_j (1 - x_j^2) of degree 2r, and reduction does not raise degree, so k >= 2r. Literature: the hyperplane case is Alon-Fueredi 1993 (covering all but one point of a grid needs sum(|S_i| - 1) hyperplanes; here 2r). The extension from subspaces to hyperplanes is immediate. Related: Jamison 1977, Brouwer-Schrijver 1978 (blocking sets, k(q-1)+1). Alon-Fueredi and Brouwer-Schrijver were seen via search results; the Jamison citation is not verified at the source. K is therefore classical, not new. (3) Use: an exact per-stage optimum for the Gauss control. At stage t (eliminated set E) the projection of S = {Ax = b} has codimension rank A - rank A_E, so the optimum is 2(rank A - rank A_E), or 1 if S is empty. (4) Owner restriction of Fable's conclusion, accepted. 'Prism gives no extra simplification' is proved for the full pinned colouring set. For the positive representation, every projection creates only per-component shifts of G[T] (17915 R3). For the complement representation and arbitrary elimination orders it is not proved. The identification of existence elimination with dag-like resolution over linear equations mod 3 needs a formal simulation (F3-valued vs 0/1 variables); not done. (5) Status: prism lattice theorem algebraically checked by PI and owner; U_H with gadget variables is an exact identity; merging calculus is incomplete (17917); K is exact and classical. A proof of P != NP remains open. No obligation closed.
