## Statement / proof text (verbatim)

NORMALIZATION: SCOPE OF REVERSIBILITY AND THE THREE PROPERTIES (owner 2026-11-27; PI derivation; CLAIMED, Claude family). (1) Exact for the block constructions. The t-copy family (t copies per vertex, y_1 = ... = y_t, edges between all copy pairs; primal graph G[K_t], tw = t(tw(G)+1) - 1) preserves U_G by the monomial substitution X_{v,c} -> prod_i X_{v,i,c}. Hybrid elimination (17919) removes the linear block equations by substitution in polynomial time and returns G. The prism pipeline Prism(G) -> affine normal form -> block encoding -> coordinate change -> G is exact and polynomial (17921-17923). w_sem = tw(G)+1 throughout (17924). B(b,t) = t b and its mixed difference 1 are arithmetic of a bilinear formula with no complexity content. (2) Owner restriction: 'every semantics-preserving structural growth can be reversed efficiently' is NOT proved in general. It is proved only for these block constructions. (3) Three properties to keep apart: (a) semantic equivalence of two representations (same solution set or same U_G up to substitution); (b) existence of a normal form; (c) efficient computability of it. The prism construction has all three. (4) PI statement for general representations (folklore-level, no citation): let c map constraint representations to representations such that c(phi) = c(psi) iff phi and psi have the same solution set. A polynomial-time canonical form c exists iff P = NP. If c is polynomial-time, then phi is unsatisfiable iff c(phi) = c(false), so the coNP-complete problem UNSAT is in P. Conversely, under P = NP take c(phi) = the lexicographically first representation of size <= |phi| equivalent to phi. It is found by prefix search over a Sigma_2 predicate and is polynomial-time because PH = P. So efficient canonical normalization of general representations is another equivalent form of the hypothesis (family of 17920, Proposition E), and it relativizes. Existence (b) holds trivially by this definition; the content lies in (c) and in restricting the admissible operations. (5) Open question, precisely stated: for a fixed admissible class N (block-local bijections, linear substitution, existential elimination of locally determined variables), which structural invariants of a representation survive every composition of operations from N? Candidate: w_sem of the nonlinear residual. Its invariance under N is not proved. A positive answer measures what the mathematics represents, not decision complexity. No obligation closed.
