## Statement / proof text (verbatim)

LEMMA W - status corrected (owner review 2026-11-19): it is the EASY direction of Hlineny-Whittle, 'Matroid tree-width', Theorem 3.2 (tree-width of a graph G with an edge equals the tree-width of its cycle matroid M(G)), restated for represented matroids: rows with supports as cliques of the coefficient graph instead of edges as cliques of G. Definition checked against the paper (final version, fi.muni.cz/~hlineny/papers/matr-tw-final.pdf, eq. (MW)): node width = sum_i r_M(E - F_i) - (d-1) r(M), exactly as used. The argument (map each element to a bag containing its support; ranks of the branch complements bounded by bag size) is the standard one; treat it as a straightforward / folklore generalisation, NOT as a new or 'proposed' result. The +1 (rank of a bag's coordinate vectors is <= |bag|, vs |bag| - 1 for a spanning forest of a graphic bag) is the only difference to the graphic case. A source stating the matrix version explicitly was not found in a short search (Cunningham-Geelen IPCO 2007 / ILP branch-width literature is the likely place).
