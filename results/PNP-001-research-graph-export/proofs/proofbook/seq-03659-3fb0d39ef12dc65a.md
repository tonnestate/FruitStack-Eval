OWNER DEEP RESEARCH 2026-11-19 (interface entropy -> P vs NP) with director source check: (a) no universal transfer from treewidth/separators/NOF to arbitrary algorithms; NOF -> ACC0 only (Hastad-Goldmann, Beigel-Tarui); P not in ACC0 does not give P != NC1. (b) Owner counterexample (exact): D_r = AND_{i<j} EXACT1(a,y_i,y_j) has treewidth r and is SAT (a=1, y=0); glued to F8 via a (GF(3) solution shifted so a=1, y=0) it is GF(3)-consistent, UNSAT, connected, of unbounded treewidth, with a constant-size core - large treewidth of a formula does not imply hardness (the family is refuted in poly time by bounded-width resolution). (c) Sources checked by the director on the web: ETH refutation claims by OpenAI and Anthropic (late Oct 2026, ~2^{n/(log n)^{1/2+o(1)}} for fixed-width k-SAT/#SAT) are widely reported (TechCrunch 2026-10-27, Fortnow blog) but independent verification is still in progress; Lean is reported for OpenAI, for Anthropic unconfirmed. Chukhin-Gajulapalli-Golovnev-Mihajlin (arXiv 2611.07972, ECCC TR26-313) confirmed: unconditional NE not in SIZE(2^n/(2n)) OR VP0_Z != VNP0_Z. Mishra-Santhanam 4n-O(log n) general-circuit bound: found only as a STOC 2027 submission claim (depends on Li s affine dispersers) - unverified. (d) Evaluation rule: algorithmic results also count through proved algorithm-to-lower-bound transfers (Williams; PIT), added to the research brief; a 2^{o(n)} k-SAT algorithm alone is no P-time algorithm and does not by itself give a new circuit lower bound (CNF lower bounds are already known).
