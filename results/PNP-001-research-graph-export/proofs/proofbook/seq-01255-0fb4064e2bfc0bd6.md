## Statement / proof text (verbatim)

D-020: The D-013 obstruction (P4 minor => not a matroid port) also occurs for 2-SAT, a polynomial-time (NL-complete) problem: 2-SAT on 3 variables with (x0|~x1),(x0|x1),(~x1|x2) present and a=(~x0|~x1), b=(~x0|x1), c=(~x0|~x2), d=(~x0|x2) free has UNSAT = ab|bc|cd (exhaustive minor search lab/transfer/p4_control.py; hand-checked by an independent referee). Calibrations: no P4 in 1in3 n=5 (GF(3) port) or XOR-SAT n=4 (GF(2) port). So "not a matroid port" holds for 2-SAT for all n >= 3 and cannot imply any super-linear circuit lower bound; D-013 measures field-linearity (it separates 1in3 only from XOR-SAT, a GF(2) port by construction), not hardness. D-013 stays a theorem about ideal secret sharing / ideal monotone span programs and is RETIRED as a proof route. Also proved (T45): the owner's subfunction bound S >= log2 D_k - 1 is true but capped at N-1 for every f; direct-sum amplification of fixed obstructions is capped at O(N); exact transfer lemma (superpolynomial infinitely often suffices; P-uniform lower bound is equivalent to P != NP). 2026-11-18
