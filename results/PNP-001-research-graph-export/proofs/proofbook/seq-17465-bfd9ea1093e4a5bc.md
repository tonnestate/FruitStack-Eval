## Statement / proof text (verbatim)

SUBSPACE SIEVE for positive 1-in-3 SAT (owner 2026-11-27; owner proof, PI re-derivation and check; CLAIMED, Claude family only). Setup: weighted T41 matrix B(X) over char 2, joint Schur (17432): K = [[A,D],[D^T,0]] on T u R, |R| = nu. (1) rank D < nu => unsatisfiable. (2) Q = basis of the left kernel of D (|T| x r, r = |T|-nu), C = Q^T A Q, q_i = rows of Q. K_U invertible <=> [[C,Q_U^T],[Q_U,0]] invertible (both <=> rank Q_U = |U| and C nondegenerate on ker Q_U). Consequences: h with dependent rows q_i (i in E_h) is never true; h,g whose row spans do not form a direct sum are never true together (this also encodes E_h disjointness). (3) Char 2: det[[C,L^T],[L,0]] = sum_{|I|=k} det(L[:,I])^2 det C[complement of I]; hence with W = span of admissible rows, s = dim W, a basis change putting W on y_1..y_s: F satisfiable <=> Z(lambda) = [y_1...y_s] sqrt(det(J_s + Y'CY')) prod_h (1 + lambda_h prod_{i in E_h} l_i(y)) is nonzero. (4) Algorithm: random weights and lambda in a polynomial-size field of char 2, coefficient by sum over 2^s subsets plus interpolation in t; time O*(2^s), polynomial space, one-sided error <= (m+|H|)/|F| (only one satisfying selection must survive). Portfolio O*(2^{min(|H|, s, d)}), d = F_3 nullity of the clause matrix (algorithm R). Check: sieve_check.py, 90 random instances (occurrences <= 4): algorithm = brute force 90/90, no false positive or negative, direct formula 90/90; s < |H| in 2/90. Placement: instance of determinantal sieving (Eiben-Koana-Wahlstroem arXiv 2502.13654 Thm 5, O*(2^r), char 2) adapted to symmetric matrices with diagonal; novelty of the parameter s unchecked. Extreme case every variable exactly 3 times: B=0, nu=0, C=0, s=m=n (RX3C, NP-complete): no saving. Random RX3C: F_3 nullity 1-3 in 600/600 (n=30,60,120). No lower bound, no P vs NP statement.
