## Statement / proof text (verbatim)

Cheraghchi-Hirahara-Myrisiotis-Yoshida: Thm 1 (corollary of MMW19): there is mu > 0 such that MCSP[2^{mu n}] not in DTIME1[N^1.01] implies P != NP. Thm 2: there is a constant mu < 1 (close to 1) with MCSP[2^{mu n}] not in BPTIME1[N^1.99]. Thm 3: for every oracle O and 1/2 < mu < 1, MCSP[2^{mu n}] not in BPTIME1^O[N^{1+mu'}] with queries of length N^{o(1)}. Thm 20: the Forbes-Kelley PRG is local, seed O~(sqrt t + log 1/eps) * log q, fooling q-state time-t one-tape DTMs (via Viola's decomposition into ROBPs of width exp(O(sqrt t log tq))). The gap to P != NP is the size parameter 2^{(1-o(1))n} vs 2^{o(n)} (authors, lines 160-165).
