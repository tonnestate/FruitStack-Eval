## Statement / proof text (verbatim)

Pitfall for the block-rank-1 cap question (is M_1(6,p) <= 20 = C(6,3)?): the naive argument 'every entry (u,v) lies in some R_j, so A = sum of pieces and rank(A) <= sum_j rank(A|R_j) <= 20' is INVALID, because the R_j = {u: j not in u} x {v: j in v} overlap and the pieces of a partition are not rectangles (a sub-pattern of a rank-1 block can have higher rank). In general Gal's rank measure is NOT capped by the number of variables: Gal 2001 (Comput. Complexity 10, characterization of span program size) is reported to give n^Omega(log n) bounds with it (secondary-level, not checked here). A cap at 20 for this domain therefore needs a domain-specific argument or an exhaustive/certified computation, not subadditivity.
