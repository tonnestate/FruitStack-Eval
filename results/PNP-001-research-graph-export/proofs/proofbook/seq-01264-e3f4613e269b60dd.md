## Statement / proof text (verbatim)

D-014m: STAR CAP (generalizes D-014f): if every clause block A|R_j is a star (its support lies in one row or one column, its "centre" c_j), then rank(A) <= k = C(n,3), over every field. Proof: a nonzero entry (u,v) lies in some R_j, hence in the support of the star A|R_j, so u = c_j or v = c_j; the <= k centres are lines covering every nonzero entry, so the term rank, and hence the rank, is <= k (Konig). Proof given by the referee (claude-haiku) in cycle RC-d0db1c00, checked by the director. Realized star constructions (cycle RC-d0db1c00, not independently recomputed): 1in3 n=5 GF(3) ratio 10 = the TRUE mSP_GF3 = 10 (D-012) - the first Gal construction in PNP-001 that reaches the exact value (previous best 5/2); 1in3 n=6 GF(3) ratio 19 (below D-013's 21, so no improvement); XOR n=5 GF(2) ratio 19 (known <= 20; control non-degenerate: 1,025,439 UNSAT inputs). Consequence: star blocks can never pass k; beating k needs blocks of rank 1 whose support is NOT a star (two disjoint nonzeros) - consistent with D-014g's open case. 2026-11-18
