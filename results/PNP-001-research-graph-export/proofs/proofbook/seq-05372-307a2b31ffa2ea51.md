## Statement / proof text (verbatim)

For positive 1-in-3 systems with m clauses and n variables, the only way a GF(3)-consistent system can be 0/1-unsatisfiable at small size is full row rank r = m. When r = m, the GF(3) solution set is a coset of ker(A) of dimension n - m, i.e. 3^(n-m) points, and the blind question reduces to whether this coset contains a 0/1 point. At n=8, m=5 the coset has 27 points.
