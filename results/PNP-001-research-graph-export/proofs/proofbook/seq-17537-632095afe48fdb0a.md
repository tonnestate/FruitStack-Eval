## Statement / proof text (verbatim)

MIS GENERATORS OF THE KEMPE SPACE (owner 2026-11-27; PI check mis_span_check.py: 255 random graphs n<=8, rank W = rank gens = rank union, 0 mismatches; CLAIMED, Claude family only). G 3-colourable; I_G = {I independent, G-I bipartite}; M_G = inclusion-maximal members (= maximal independent sets of G with bipartite complement); R_G = {v: exists M in M_G, v in M, M-v in I_G}. THEOREM: W_G = span_Q({3*1_M - 1 : M in M_G} u {e_v : v in R_G}). PROOF: (a) W_G = span{3*1_I - 1 : I in I_G}: every I in I_G is a colour class (2-colour G-I); for classes A,B,C of a colouring 3*1_A-1 = (1_A-1_B)+(1_A-1_C), each a sum of Kempe vectors b_K; conversely b_K = 1_{K cap A}-1_{K cap B} = 1_A - 1_{A'} with A' the class after the swap, = ((3*1_A-1)-(3*1_{A'}-1))/3. (b) For I in I_G extend to a maximal independent M; G-M is an induced subgraph of G-I, so M in M_G; for v in M-I, M-v contains I, so M-v in I_G and v in R_G; 3*1_I-1 = (3*1_M-1) - 3*sum_{v in M-I} e_v. (c) 3e_v = (3*1_M-1)-(3*1_{M-v}-1) for v in R_G. QED. ALGORITHM: enumerate maximal independent sets with polynomial delay and polynomial space (Tsukiyama et al. 1977, from memory, verify at source), at most 3^{n/3} (Moon-Moser 1965); test bipartiteness of G-M and of G-(M-v); keep an incremental Q-basis (<= n vectors, Bareiss). With one colouring from any M in M_G, aff(Sol01(F(G))) = x_chi + W_G (x) U (17497). Time O*(3^{n/3}) = O*(1.4423^n), polynomial space. STRENGTH: exponential upper bound only; same enumeration as Lawler's 1976 3-colouring algorithm (from memory, verify), here lifted to the span. Hull computation is NP-hard (17497 (2)), so no polynomial version is expected. No lower bound, no obligation closed, no P vs NP progress.
