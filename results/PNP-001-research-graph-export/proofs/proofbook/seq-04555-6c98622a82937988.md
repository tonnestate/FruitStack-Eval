## Statement / proof text (verbatim)

For positive 1-in-3 formulas where every variable occurs in exactly two clauses, the clause graph is a cubic multigraph (clauses are vertices, variables are edges). The formula is SAT iff the graph has a perfect matching. By Petersen's 1891 theorem, every bridgeless cubic graph has a perfect matching, so the formula is SAT whenever the graph is bridgeless.
