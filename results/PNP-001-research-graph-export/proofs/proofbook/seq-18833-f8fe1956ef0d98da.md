T68 (PI, 2026-11-28; CLAIMED, Claude family; theory/T68-structural-certificate-architecture-2026-11-28.md). Owner architecture: fixed L in EXPTIME\P, alternating poly-space machine (APSPACE=EXPTIME, CKS), poly-bit clock, certificate C with root/OR/AND/terminal conditions. Prop 1: soundness/completeness by induction on depth (checked on 300 random AND/OR DAGs); the winning set is always a certificate, so only circuit size matters. Prop 2: certificate predicate is Pi_1, existence of poly-size C is Sigma_2, and 'every x in L has a poly-size certificate' (P*) implies L in Sigma_2P. Cor 3: H and P* contradict the time hierarchy, so the final steps of the chain are correct, but any derivation of P* from H is already a proof of not-H: the open link is the theorem itself (unconditionally P* for EXPTIME-complete L gives EXPTIME=PSPACE, open). Prop 4: relativization barrier - with a PSPACE-complete oracle A, P^A=NP^A while EXPTIME^A != P^A (time hierarchy relativizes, CKS relativizes), so P*^A fails; no black-box use of H can yield P*; a non-relativizing use of A's own transcripts is required. Positive content: the certificate needs only a sandwich R <= C <= W (reachable-under-a-winning-strategy set R, inductive-invariant/Craig-interpolant relaxation), which is weaker than the exact compression of W or F^T that T66 Thm 10.6 bounds below by T^(1/kappa^2) under H. Obligations O1-O4 in the note.
