(1) Owner identity [x=v][y=u] = [x=u][y=v] - phi(x,y) phi(u,v), phi(x,y) = [x != y](-1)^x, verified on all 16 points; it is the lambda = 1 (two-square) instance of the Anthropic decomposition; the correction for weights A, B is exactly -(A(0,1)-A(1,0))(B(0,1)-B(1,0)) and vanishes if one weight is symmetric. (2) STRUCTURE: the Lemma-19 sum M = sum_alpha P_alpha is a PATH (one-dimensional) contraction: each constraint is an equality of crossing sequences at ONE cut between ADJACENT blocks; no two constraints cross, so the crossing identity has nothing to act on unless the chain is first re-embedded; Anthropic's rewriting removes the cost of crossings (expansion), while a path network is already contracted at cost poly(bond dimension) - and the bond dimension w = exp(Theta(sqrt t log tq)) (crossing-sequence information) is exactly the CHMY bottleneck. (3) Any signed rewriting of M into K terms, each a width-w ROBP in its own order, analysed term-generically, is covered by JOINT-CONTROL-FLOOR seq 17284 (1_H is a disjoint OR = signed sum with coefficients 1), so it needs log(wK) <= N^{mu+o(1)}. (4) QUANTITATIVE: the published gain of the mechanism is a factor Theta(log n / loglog n) in the exponent; the CHMY transfer needs a POLYNOMIAL factor N^{0.495} (from sqrt t log tq ~ N^{0.505} to N^{o(1)}). VERDICT: no transfer at the needed strength is visible; not excluded as a statement, but the crossing identity does not address the CHMY bottleneck. What a Weg-1 construction must reduce is the crossing-sequence information itself (bond dimension of the path decomposition), using q = O(1) beyond counting (seq 17279 (c), 17284, 17286).
