## Statement / proof text (verbatim)

GRAPH-NATIVE TRANSFER OF THE CUT-FACTOR STEP FAILS FOR BOOLEAN COMPUTATION (PI 2026-11-27; CLAIMED, Claude family; standard fact). Graph: n disjoint 2-paths a_i-b_i-c_i; edge variables x_i={a_i,b_i}, y_i={b_i,c_i}. f(G) = parity of the number of i with both edges present (= inner product IP_n). Boolean circuit size O(n) (n AND + n-1 XOR). For the balanced edge cut {x} | {y}, the communication matrix has rank 2^n - 1 (0/1) and 2^n (+-1), checked numerically for n=2..8 (scratch ip_rank.py). Hence 'small computation on graph G implies low rank across balanced edge cuts' is false for Boolean gates. The OpenAI step exists_balanced_cutFactors needs homogeneous degree and parse grammar (sums of products), not graph structure. Consequence: a graph-native version of the geometric contradiction must either restrict to monotone/positive operations (then it is the arithmetic case of 17561, and Razborov's monotone bound for perfect matching, from memory, shows such bounds also hold for problems in P) or use a graph invariant that is NOT bounded by cut rank. No obligation closed.
