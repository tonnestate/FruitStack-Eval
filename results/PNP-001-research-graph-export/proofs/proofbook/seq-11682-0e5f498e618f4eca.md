## Statement / proof text (verbatim)

The R1 class (F_2-linear/affine staged realizations) is too restrictive to capture general Search-MCSP streaming algorithms. The SEARCH bound S ≥ 2^m holds because the totality requirement (outputting a circuit for YES instances) forces the kernel to be trivial. For the DECISION version, the bound S ≥ N - d_s is weak for small m (d_s > N) but becomes nontrivial for m ≥ 7. To approach the full (8) target, we must consider: (R2) F_3-linear/affine realizations, (R3) nonlinear recodings with bounded treewidth, (R4) general staged circuits with Sigma3SAT^A oracle transitions as in the manuscript. The next step is to formulate and prove the F_3-linear/affine analog.
