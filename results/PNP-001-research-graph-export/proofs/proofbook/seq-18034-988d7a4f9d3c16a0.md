Lemma1: R(1-A)=0 alone is vacuous (A=1 satisfies it); the existence direction is also needed.

Lemma3: Phi_W != 0 in F[G,x] for every field and every polynomial W (integral domain; a non-member G gives 1-R(G,W(G)) != 0). Correctness is therefore always Boolean cancellation (Phi_W in I minus {0}), never formal; checking it is coNP-complete (T48 3b); its IPS size equals that of the gate system and the Lemma-B/GF(3) images up to additive O(s) (T48 9.3/9.4); existence of short certificates is not forced (9.5c).
Lemma4: consequences of (*) that use W only as a black box and R only through its low-degree extension hold in the AW world (17590), so they are algebrizing and give no contradiction. A contradiction needs a white-box property Pi of ALL poly-size circuits W with (*) whose negation is proved unconditionally; such an argument simultaneously proves NP not in P/poly (as a consequence, not a premise). NF(R(G,W(G))) = NF(chi_L) adds nothing.
First unproved implication (sharpened, not a small step): a white-box law 'W poly-size with (*) => Pi(W)' with not-Pi proved. Same gap as 17920/17929, in its exact form.
