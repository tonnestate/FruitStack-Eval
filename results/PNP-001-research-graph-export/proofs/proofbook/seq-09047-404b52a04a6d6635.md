## Statement / proof text (verbatim)

Let X and Z be finite sets, and let E = {E_1,...,E_k} (k >= 1) be relations E_i subset of X x Z with projection pi: X x Z -> X. Let H = pi(intersection of all E_i) be nonempty. Assume COND: if k >= 2 then for every j in {1,...,k}, pi(intersection of E_i over i != j) = X. Then every nonempty subset T of E with intersection of T nonempty satisfies pi(intersection of T) in {H, X}.
