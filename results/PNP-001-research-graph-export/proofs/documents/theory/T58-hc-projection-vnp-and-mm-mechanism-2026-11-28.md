## A. Polynomials (corrected)
Graph G, reference triangle R0,R1,R2. Colour indicators e_vc (one-hot), weights X_vc.
- Fully weighted: W(G;X)=Σ_{c:V→{0,1,2} proper} ∏_v X_{v,c(v)}  (monomials = colourings; distinct colourings ⇒ distinct monomials).
- Pinned: P_j (c(R_i)=σ_j(i) fixed), obtained from W by X_{R_i,c}=[c=σ(i)].
- Unpinned U=Σ_σ P_σ = 2(P_0+P_1+P_2)-type sum over triangle colourings.
Error in v1: the factor 1+e_v1(y_v−1) does not separate colours 0 and 2. For an unpinned graph with ≥1 edge the swap 0↔2 is a fixed-point-free involution on colourings with identical monomials ⇒ the polynomial is 2·(…) and VANISHES in characteristic 2. Correct factor: ∏_{c=0}^{2}(1−e_vc+e_vc X_vc). The edge-validity factor (1−e_uc e_vc) is Boolean-valued only under one-hot (oh_v=1); off one-hot it is a polynomial value, not a Boolean. [P]
Consequences: any statement "U_G is VNP-complete over every field" is only valid for the pinned/fully weighted W (monomial-injective, no char-2 collapse); for unpinned U only char≠2 or after pinning. VNP membership: W(G;X)=Σ_{e∈{0,1}^{3n}} oh(e)·∏_{edges,c}(1−e_uc e_vc)·∏_{v,c}(1−e_vc+e_vc X_vc), a Boolean-witness sum of a poly-size formula, hence in VNP over every field. [P]

## B. Models (not to be conflated)
F formal polynomial arithmetic; E evaluation on {0,1}-inputs over a field F_q; S Boolean semiring (∨,∧); B general Boolean circuits.
Valid: F-circuit ⇒ E-circuit ⇒ (fixed F_p) B-circuit of comparable size; monotone N-circuit ⇒ S. Lower bounds go in the opposite order: LB(B)⇒LB(E)⇒LB(F). Invalid: F-upper ⇒ S-upper (cancellation/subtraction/finite-field identities, e.g. Per=Det over F₂, Razborov monotone matching n^{Ω(log n)} [EXT, from memory]). Hence NO Boolean-circuit lower bound follows from any formal lower bound; the missing step is E vs F = T50's MT (⇔ VP=VNP). [P]
