## Findings
1. The paper's Prop 7.3 as written depends on the external Severi-dimension theorems (Christ–He–Tyomkin, Tyomkin). The independent
   audit read them and found the use correct.
2. The Lean development does NOT formalize Prop 7.3 as written. It proves a weaker effective codimension bound,
   floor(C(q+2,2)/18) ~ k²/324 (`critical_dimension_effective`, `spectral_effective_bound`: 18n + T <= 9(k+1)(k+2)), takes
   d = floor(T/18), and still reaches the same headline constant: (r-1)(d-1) >= k³/1000 and m <= 11k+10 give
   m³/(5 470 524 e) >= m³/(5 529 600 e). So Theorem 1.1 with its stated constants does not need the Severi theorems; the paper's
   stronger intermediate statement (d = k²/100, k³/(3200e)) does.
3. Slack noted by the audit (not errors): Prop 6.1 ignores the k fixed points on u = 0 (k more codimension); q >= (k-3)/3 is
   slightly sharper than the paper's (k-4)/3.
4. Small-k behaviour of the gradient locus: for k = 4 it has dimension 2 in 16 variables (codim 14), far more than the vacuous
   T-k = -1. k = 5: computing the gradient locus (25 quadrics in 25 variables over GF(2^8)) did not finish in 30 min; not pursued.

## Consequence
Within what was checked, Theorem 1.1 (border dc(per_m) >= m³/(5 529 600 e), m >= 1408) stands. The comparison baseline for PNP-001 is
cubic. Remaining unverified: the Lean build itself, and the authors' Section 8 polar persistence argument beyond the reading in T15.
