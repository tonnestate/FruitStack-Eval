## Q1. A proved negative transfer: no characteristic-2 structure of T41 can separate
perm_m ≡ det_m (mod 2) as polynomials (every sign is +1). Over 𝔽₂ (or 𝔽̄₂), ℓ^{n−m}·perm_m is therefore a projection of det_n already
for n = m, by the same padding as in characteristic 0. So the characteristic-2 analogue of every containment question in T11 is
answered POSITIVELY, and every characteristic-2 computation of the T11 quantities sees no separation.
Consequence: a separating multiplicity obstruction is a characteristic-0 (or odd-characteristic) phenomenon. The T41 tools that
live in characteristic 2 cannot contribute separating information:
- D4/D5 determinants over 𝔽₂;
- Björklund's Lemma 4.2;
- the 2-group fixed-point reduction FP mod 2;
- the GL₂-matchgate statements, which concern Pfaffians that coincide with determinants mod 2.
Only tools that are valid in characteristic 0 or modulo odd primes can transfer.

## Q2. A positive transfer: fixed-point certificates for the HARD HALF (lower bounds on γ_λ)
T11-P certifies γ_λ ≥ 1 by evaluating a highest-weight function P_T (from a semistandard tableau T) at the point
f = ℓ^{n−m}·perm_m. P_T(f) is an exponential SIGNED SUM over column assignments: 829,440,000 terms for λ = (6,6,4,4,4). Its
nonvanishing is the certificate.
T41's MOD-P / FP principle transfers verbatim to such sums. Let H be a finite group acting on the set of terms, preserving each
term's value INCLUDING its sign, with |H| = p^k, p prime. Then
    P_T(f) ≡ Σ_{terms fixed by H} (term value)   (mod p),
because non-trivial H-orbits have size divisible by p and constant value.
- If the fixed-term sum is ≢ 0 mod p, then P_T(f) ≠ 0, hence γ_λ ≥ 1 (and, with several points and tableaux, γ_λ ≥ rank of the
  evaluation matrix mod p, a lower bound because rank cannot rise under reduction).
- Sources of H: the symmetry group of perm_m (S_m × S_m ⋊ ℤ/2 and diagonal tori with entries ±1 or roots of unity), acting on the
  tensor entries of f, combined with symbol permutations of T that fix its column multiset. The twin-colour lemma of T11-P is the
  special case of an involution (p = 2) that reverses sign. That is exactly why it ANNIHILATES pairs instead of preserving them.
- What this gives: a potentially much cheaper, independently checkable certificate of NONVANISHING. That is the expensive half
  of T11-B (obstacle T11-A.2 concerns certificate cost).
- What it does NOT give: an upper bound U on δ_λ. Vanishing on D_n needs proofs such as Lemma A or rank arguments. Fixed points
  certify non-zero, never zero. And nothing here produces L > U by itself.
Status of Q2: the congruence is the T41 FP principle; its application to tableau sums at padded-permanent points is NOT yet
carried out, and the needed sign-preservation of a concrete H on the term set is NOT yet verified for any λ.

## Q3. A small model of T11's central difficulty inside T41 (orbit vs closure)
T11's core difficulty is that closure multiplicities differ from orbit multiplicities (γ_λ ≤ a_λ). The T41 matchgate no-go
theorems (MG-3, GL2-NoGo, certified in §14f) are ORBIT statements: the Gröbner certificates contain z(ad − bc) − 1, i.e.
invertible T only. The closure (border) version is open and well-posed: is there a curve T(ε) ∈ GL₂ with projective limits of
T(ε)^{⊗3}·EXACT1₃ and of (T(ε)⁻ᵀ)^{⊗d}·EQ_d that are matchgates simultaneously, while keeping the network value
(leading-coefficient issue as for border rank)?
Observation (proved by explicit curve): the projective orbit closure of EQ_d = x^d + y^d under the variable-side action contains
x^{d−1}y, i.e. the matchgate [0, 1, 0, …, 0]. Take x ↦ x + εy, y ↦ ζx with ζ^d = −1: (x + εy)^d − x^d = dε·x^{d−1}y + O(ε²).
Whether the clause side can be driven to a matchgate ALONG THE SAME CURVE, and whether the network value survives, is not yet
examined. This is a finite-dimensional toy of "closure strictly larger than orbit", with exact tools available
(Singular, matchgate_miner.py). It is offered as the next small, rigorous step that exercises T11's real difficulty, not as a
P vs NP step.
