# T11-O — The Padding-Floor Characterization of ker E_Y (PI + FruitStack, 2026-11-17)

An experiment-derived candidate general theorem about the padded-permanent orbit closure. Produced by the intelligent-search loop:
the (4^5) experiment at d=5 suggested it; the falsification-prioritized search confirmed it; one direction is proven.

## Statement
Let Y_{m,n} = closure(GL_{n²}·(z^{n-m}·perm_m)) ⊂ Sym^n(W), W=C^{n²}, z a padding linear form independent of the m² permanent
variables. For a weight-λ highest weight vector P_λ of degree d (λ ⊢ nd):
    **P_λ|_{Y_{m,n}} ≡ 0   ⟺   λ_1 < d(n-m).**

## Proof of (⟸) [Lemma A — REPLACED 2026-11-28 by an equivariant proof; the old coordinate argument had a gap]
**Gap in the earlier proof (kept for the record).** It restricted f to a k-plane U and chose coordinates with z|_U = u_1, then evaluated a *highest-weight vector* P_λ. A highest-weight vector is tied to a fixed
Borel/coordinate system. The set of restrictions {f|_U} is GL(U)-stable, but vanishing of P_λ on the sub-family with z|_U = u_1 gives vanishing of P_λ only on that sub-family; to reach all of Y one needs the whole GL(U)-module generated by P_λ
to vanish there, and the weight count Σ_j α^(j)_1 = λ_1 holds only for the weight vectors of x_1-weight λ_1, not for all vectors of the module. The pigeonhole step is therefore not justified as written.
**Equivariant proof.** Let W = ℂ^{n²}, Y' := closure{ℓ^{n−m}·g : ℓ ∈ W, g ∈ Sym^m W} ⊂ Sym^n W (GL(W)-stable, contains the padded permanent orbit, so Y_{m,n} ⊆ Y'). The map φ : W × Sym^m W → Sym^n W, φ(ℓ,g) = ℓ^{n−m}g, is
GL(W)-equivariant (diagonal action) with dense image in Y'. A degree-d polynomial on Sym^n W pulls back to a polynomial of bidegree (d(n−m), d) in (ℓ,g), so φ* : ℂ[Sym^n W]_d → Sym^{d(n−m)}W^* ⊗ Sym^d(Sym^m W^*) is GL(W)-equivariant, and its kernel is I(Y')_d. Hence ℂ[Y']_d ↪ Sym^{a}W^* ⊗ Sym^d(Sym^m W^*), a := d(n−m), as GL(W)-modules, and ℂ[Y_{m,n}]_d is a quotient of ℂ[Y']_d.
So S_λW^* ⊂ ℂ[Y_{m,n}]_d ⇒ S_λ W^* occurs in Sym^a W^* ⊗ ⨁_μ S_μ W^*(multiplicity p_{μ,m,d}) (μ ⊢ dm). By the Pieri rule S_λ ⊂ Sym^a ⊗ S_μ ⇔ **λ/μ is a horizontal strip of size a**: λ_1 ≥ μ_1 ≥ λ_2 ≥ μ_2 ≥ … .
Then a = |λ| − |μ| = Σ_i (λ_i − μ_i) ≤ Σ_i (λ_i − λ_{i+1}) = λ_1 (using μ_i ≥ λ_{i+1}, λ_{ℓ+1} = 0). Hence **λ_1 ≥ d(n−m)**, equivalently λ_1 < d(n−m) ⇒ P_λ|_Y ≡ 0. ∎
*Conventions checked:* ℂ[Sym^n W] = Sym(Sym^n W^*), so highest-weight vectors of degree d lie in S_λW^* with λ ⊢ nd; |λ| = d(n−m) + dm ✓; consistent with the (3,4,5) data (the only vanishing case (4,4,4,4,4), λ_1 = 4 < 5).
**Strengthened by-product (new, same proof):** γ_λ(Y_{m,n}) ≤ Σ_μ p_{μ,m,d}·[λ/μ horizontal strip of size d(n−m)] — a necessary-occurrence upper bound sharper than the floor alone — and ℓ(λ) ≤ ℓ(μ)+1 ≤ dm+1 (the essential-variable bound ℓ(λ) ≤ m²+1 below is stronger and independent).
**Lemma B ("edge reduction") used the same coordinate choice and is NOT proved**; it was only a route to the converse, which is refuted below, so nothing depends on it.
