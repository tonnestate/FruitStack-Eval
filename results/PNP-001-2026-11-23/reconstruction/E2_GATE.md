## Exact E2(F)
Fix one field F throughout E1, E2 and Theorem 12; for computational interpretations use an explicitly specified effective constant encoding. Let Psi_n be the exact doubly lifted non-3-colorability CNF in THEOREM-12.md lines 7–12; Q_j clause falsification products, B_i=X_i^2-X_i. IPS_F(Psi_n) is the minimum arithmetic gate count of a division-free general DAG certificate C_n(X,Y,Z) with formal identities C_n(X,0,0)=0 and C_n(X,Q_n(X),B_n(X))=1. Fan-in two + and multiplication, field constants, arbitrary sharing, no assumed degree or placeholder restriction. Align constant convention with any invoked theorem; distinguish arithmetic gate size from bit-size.

E2(F): P=NP implies there exist k,K,n0 (k>=1, K>0) such that every n>=n0 has IPS_F(Psi_n)<=K*n^k. Quantifiers are for a fixed F; no uniformity across fields is asserted.

E2(F2): the same proposition with all certificate constants and operations in F2. This is a specialization, not automatically a strengthening of the universally quantified E2(F). A result for an arbitrary single other field does not by itself prove E2(F2). In characteristic two, extension of scalars preserves an F2 certificate and its identities; descent in the other direction requires a separate argument and cost accounting. No cross-characteristic transfer assumed. No chosen field is mandated by the statement of Theorem 12.

## Relationship to known open questions
Cook–Reckhow: NP=coNP iff some propositional proof system is polynomially bounded. This is not a claim about IPS. Grochow–Pitassi, JACM 2018, Theorem 1.2 and Lemma 4.2, https://www.cs.toronto.edu/~toni/Papers/jacm-gp.pdf, construct VNP certificates and obtain VP!=VNP from superpolynomial IPS lower bounds with the applicable field conventions. Contrapositively VP=VNP supplies polynomial IPS certificates for the relevant CNF families. No P=NP -> VP=VNP transfer established here. Grochow 2023, https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.02184, proves relationships between PIT in NP and simulation of IPS by a Cook–Reckhow system; the direction does not prove that IPS simulates a polynomially bounded system. Even P=NP and polynomial-time PIT verification would not guarantee short certificates. EF simulations similarly do not establish short EF proofs under P=NP.

E2 is a family-specific conditional boundedness question. No established equivalence to a named open conjecture was identified in this targeted source check; it is not justified to call it a solved transfer or a renamed equivalent problem. A sufficient route is a family-specific small IPS certificate under P=NP, or a proved simulation of a proof system that is polynomially bounded for this family under P=NP. E2 could also be unconditional if short certificates exist, which would challenge E1 rather than establish separation.

## E1 and strategic gate
For every fixed k,K, all sufficiently large n with IPS_F(Psi_n)<=K*n^k must admit an IPS_LIN roABP certificate with polynomial width (some order). Given the exponential all-order width lower bound, such a pointwise implication is equivalent to eventual superpolynomial general IPS lower bounds for this family: the implication forces absence of polynomial-size IPS certificates at large n; conversely that absence makes the implication vacuous. Thus E1 itself is a major lower-bound problem; a constructive conversion would be stronger in methodology. Lemma 15 assumes an existing full roABP and sparse placeholder support; Theorem 14 bounds low placeholder degree; Theorem 16 treats a canonical product certificate. None supplies E1 or E2.
