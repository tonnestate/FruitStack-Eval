# Synthesis delivery checklist — acceptance, not model votes

Operational status: research remains running as far as this audit can establish; no freeze or scheduler changes. This is an audit specification, not a deployed enforcement mechanism.

A synthesis is a deliverable, not an APPROACH, a single conclusion, a FINAL label or a review vote. Preserve all historical records. For each original mission recover the exact owner/director instruction, requested deliverables, actual final output, all linked intermediate work, and their immutable identifiers. Never infer instruction compliance solely from a success status, urgency label or text length.

## Required per-synthesis acceptance fields (binary, 0/1)
- task_01: exact original instruction and mission ID recovered.
- scope_01: every requested subtask and route accounted for; no substituted objective.
- theory_01: complete theorem statements, formulas, hypotheses and quantifiers included or linked to exact immutable sources.
- chain_01: all inference steps with explicit AND dependencies, candidate OR alternatives and parameter compatibility.
- proof_01: actual mathematical derivation for every claimed established step; missing proofs explicitly identified.
- gap_01: first missing step and all further missing steps identified, with exact missing statement and available alternatives.
- evidence_01: source commit, canonical question/step ID, ledger sequence or artifact for each claim.
- review_01: statement-matched independent mathematical review or reproduced formal proof, with disputed reviews preserved.
- deliverable_01: required files actually exist and can be read; check substantive content, not filenames.
- completed_01: conjunction of the preceding checks. Zero means not established as fulfilled; distinguish FAIL, MISSING and NOT_CHECKED in reason fields.

Audit agent behavior separately: instruction omission, objective substitution, premature FINAL, missing artifact, unverifiable claim of execution, erroneous author proof, false positive/negative review, review of different statement. For each confirmed incident record instruction reference, actual response reference, specific unmet requirement, model/session, downstream consequence, and exact repair/retry task. If original instruction or output is unavailable classify NOT_CHECKED, not disobedience. A concise output can pass if its linked evidence satisfies all requirements; a long output can fail.

A failed delivery check must not be counted as a mathematical counterexample. A correct conditional implication may have local proof status 1 while global reachability is 0. A genuine open problem may remain even when the synthesis deliverable passes all checks.

## Continuation and historical coverage

For OR alternatives require identical mathematical conclusion and compatible model, field, parameters, quantifiers and size measures before treating them as interchangeable. Matching target labels alone are not a proof of equivalence. Keep alternative status 0 until compatibility is established.
